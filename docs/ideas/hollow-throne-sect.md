## Intent

A heterodox **soul sect** turned grudge-bearing shadow power. Hunted near to extinction after the Dao Wars for the secrets it held, it now holds a city of its own. Its spine is **not** a restrained Immortal but an **active threat**: the **Emperor of Souls upon the Hollow Throne**, a **Pseudo God** who can use his power as he pleases.

Gives the spirit path a world presence (PR #127 notes "no spirit sect") — reviled, hidden, dangerous.

## History

### Warring States — used by all, admitted by none *(suggestion)*

Soul cultivation was reviled, so no soul sect could stand openly. This one survived by being **useful**: spies sat in every camp, and soul arts suit the work — reading prisoners, planting watchers, possessing messengers. Every power hired them; none admitted it.

### The cleanup — a purge worse than the cult's *(owner)*

- After the Dao Wars, every power wanted its secrets buried — and this sect had **read them out of souls**.
- Purge efforts were **at or above** the effort to extirpate the Heavenly Demon Cult, because of their **wealth of secrets**.
- A good number of members were purged **in abhorrent ways** to ensure the **core** would not be found. *Suggestion:* the sect destroyed its own members' souls so they could not be soul-searched — survivors carry that.
- The sect **almost disbanded** and hid in the dark for a long, long time.

### Now — the Hollow Throne reveals itself *(owner + suggestion)*

- **Owner:** the sect now has decent weight to throw around on the board and a **city of its own**.
- *Suggestion:* holding Fengdu openly is a **present-day turning point** — possibly recent, possibly something the player lives through.

## Why they don't release the secrets

**Owner:** it's a nuclear option — monumental escalation, no repairing their status. And even if people half-believed them without proof, it's the word of a sect **hunted by everyone for public and "seemingly justifiable" reasons** against **everyone's denial**. Everyone involved wants them **dead and silent**.

**Suggestions that stack:**

1. **Proof is soul-read memory** — only soul cultivators can verify it. Undeniable only to the people it's about → a private threat, not a public weapon.
2. **Leverage only works unused** — release everything and it's worth nothing.
3. **Release unites enemies** — each power fears a *different* secret; selective threats keep them divided.
4. **The Emperor needs quiet** — his goal is Personal Godhood, not revenge on the world.
5. **Quiet use** — feeding one secret to spark a feud or steer a charter dispute (hidden-influence hook).

## The Emperor of Souls

### Why he can act *(suggestion)*

Apex beings are restrained (Immortals draw heaven's resistance; Saints are watched; Gods' souls are contested). A **Pseudo God** hasn't crossed — heaven holds nothing against him yet. He sits in the **gap in the deterrence system**: too strong for anything at Void Refinement or below, not worth an Immortal's provoking heaven, and the Tian founder sleeps.

**Mirror:** the Tian founder — a **restrained Immortal** who sleeps — versus the Emperor, **unrestrained** at the threshold, who acts.

### Arc — two paths lived, the third chosen *(owner)*

| Stage | What happened |
|-------|---------------|
| **Worshipped** | Realised his followers' **faith could enhance the soul**. |
| **Loss** | Lost his followers in the cleanup purge. His hunted period was his **weakest in a long time** — he never wants to relive it. |
| **Devouring** | After losing them, began **taking people's souls into his own**. Currently **on track as a Devouring God**. |
| **Looking inward** | Discovered there were two paths; looked inward for a **third** — to not rely on others. Wants to ascend as a **Personal God**. |

Knowledge came from **living the paths**, not from a manual *(owner)*. Either can't ascend yet or chooses not to *(owner, open)*.

### What twisted him *(owner question; suggestion)*

Both, deliberately entangled: the **betrayal and hunt** gave him the grudge; **devouring** gave him other people's fragments — voices, memories, urges. Even he can't tell which parts are his. That's why Personal Godhood is an **obsession**: reclaiming his ego means **cutting everyone else out of himself**.

**Present danger (suggestion):** he must keep devouring to survive while building toward self-sufficiency — every soul is fuel for the leap.

**"Never again" (owner → suggestion):** hoards power, builds a fortress city, trusts no one, never lets the sect grow soft or visible enough to be purged twice. The sect's fanaticism comes from him.

## Fengdu (丰都) — City of the Dead

- **Owner:** not necessarily their original city — where they **buried his followers** or **brought their souls to rest**.
- Name: **Fengdu** — the ghost city / underworld capital of Chinese folklore. *Alt:* Ghost Capital (鬼都), Necropolis, City of the Dead.
- *Suggestion:* a qi-dead or war-scarred site hides from ordinary senses; attacking it needs apex force, which is restrained.
