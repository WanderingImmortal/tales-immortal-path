## Intent

**刀道** *Dāo Dào* — **Blade Great Dao** — teachable path for **刀** *dāo* / saber / broad-blade ( **not** 剑 *jiàn* Sword Dao). Merc armies, bandits, charter guards — **common** ladder fantasy.

**Not esoteric:** player already knows **weapon + techniques + range**. At **Dao Manifestation**, the walker **wears** their blade line as **law** (显法) — “saber law” in plain English = **finished 刀道**, not a weather system.

## Rule sentence (working — can stay simple)

*The **cut** **decides**.*

Or even **path slogan only** until combat doc needs one line — Manifestation identity = **peak 刀**, not a second cosmology essay.

## Distinct from Sword Dao

| | **剑道** Sword | **刀道** Blade |
|--|----------------|----------------|
| **Sect face** | Celestial Sword — pure, aloof | No single orthodox face — **merc / field** |
| **Feel** | Line, intent, duel | **Mass**, guard break, **close mid** |
| **Fu** | Would be wrong brand | **Captain** who cut his way up |

## Player experience (why this ships)

| Layer | Player sees |

| **Power** | High **depth + fill** on blade profiles — not “domain philosophy” |
| **Fairness** | Normal win/lose; no stolen quest completion |

**F3 domain (optional, plain):** short radius **arc** — bonus **Structure** stress inside; **leave** = normal. No tick puzzle.
