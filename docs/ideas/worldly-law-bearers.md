## Intent

**Worldly Law Bearers** are **Dao Manifestation** cultivators who **wear a law** but **do not sit on a charter mountain** — **unseated**. Jianghu rumor category; **not** a faction.

They are **walking leftovers** of the **Dao War era** — **nothing** chains them to charter politeness; personalities can be **outrageous**, **strange**, **monstrous in manners** without being setting-breaking power.

## Design note — “insane” dao (owner pass)

《帝霸》**狂祖** / Insane Ancestor: **狂** = **unrestrained at civilization scale** (anti-heaven, **道源**, lineage founder), **not** random antics. Fu: **merc corps** past; **habits ≠ law**; **狂** = camp rules in peacetime (**军律大道**). See Fu doc.
