# War legends — living roster

Updated: 2026-11-28. Source: author design discussion. Private lore working document; not a player-facing ranking or implementation plan.

## Intent

Remember individuals whose feats defined the Dao Wars. The distinction is informal, not a power tier, organisation or official ranked list. Some legends may be dead, missing, retired or still active; present sect spines may have eclipsed their wartime reputations. Their extraordinary feats should follow the world's cultivation rules. Preserve core character concepts separately from tentative history.

## Soul King

### Agreed working character concept

- Male soul cultivator, late Dao Seeking equivalent during the war (exact Soul Path realm label not assigned).
- Divided his soul into nine structurally sound souls in a single procedure: an achievement closer to total disassembly than ordinary [soul shearing](soul-shearing.md).
- Nine independently functioning and cultivated souls made him immensely destructive, versatile and frustrating to fight. Concurrent techniques, defence, formations and spiritual attacks are illustrative, not a fixed combat kit.
- His ninefold division may be his greatest cultivation achievement, although history remembers his battlefield feats.
- Four or five souls were confirmed destroyed in his last recorded confrontation. The exact number is open. Given shearing backlash, his injuries were severe. He vanished; survival, remaining cultivation, whereabouts and inheritance are unknown. Do not resolve them yet.

### Tentative history

A friend of the Emperor of Souls, sympathetic to soul cultivators facing discrimination without necessarily endorsing the Soul Cult's shady practices. Membership is unresolved. He fought through friendship and obligations rather than conquest.

Preferred provisional incident: during the Dao Wars, an unnamed power retaliated against a Soul Cult establishment after discovering intelligence services benefiting its enemies. The Soul King intervened in defence of the Cult. The opposing power called in an obligation from a zither cultivator. This preserves the Cult's wartime political neutrality as an intelligence contractor while allowing self-defence; neutrality is not immunity from retaliation. Do not move organised post-war persecution into the war or establish a full-scale Cult offensive. Exact contract, location, faction and timing are open.

The preferred opponent is the unnamed Zither Dao resident of the [independent sanctuary city-state](independent-sanctuary-city-state.md), with her master as a fallback if chronology fails. She is the city's defender, not its established founder/city lord. Both fought under obligations; establishing a winner and loser was sufficient. She severely injured him, ending his participation, and departed without pursuing his death. No personal feud is required.

Resonance between souls derived from the same original soul is a possible explanation for the damage, not an established universal counter or a new rule of [Zither Dao](zither-dao.md). Sword, extreme Yang/Yin, music and soul poison were discussed as alternative avenues; poison may be too terminal for the intended outcome. Her wartime realm, exact technique and chronology remain unresolved.

## Zither cultivator — candidate war legend

Potentially a lesser-known legend remembered for a single feat rather than a long military career. Appearing, defeating her assigned opponent and leaving fits her reserved character. Her existing city history and current role remain governed by the sanctuary note. Do not establish a second identity, new master or wartime city affiliation without review.
