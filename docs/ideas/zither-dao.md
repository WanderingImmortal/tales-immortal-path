## Intent

**Zither Dao** is an overarching path concerned with **music, harmony, rhythm, expression**, and relationships between **sounds** — not generic “sound wave” physics. Exemplar resident: the sanctuary city’s strongest defender — [`independent-sanctuary-city-state.md`](independent-sanctuary-city-state.md) § Figure D (**unnamed**).

**Label legend:** **Established (canon)** · **Provisional / illustrative** · **Open / TBD**

---

## Established — principles

- **Resonance and vibration** matter, but the Dao is **not** reducible to brute sound manipulation.
- Supports **artistic** and **martial** cultivation.
- Potential applications (**not every practitioner has all**):

| Application | Note |
|-------------|------|
| Resonance / harmonics | Structural or qi coupling |
| Large-area destructive expression | e.g. bandit-routing attack (**provisional** event in sanctuary doc) |
| Disruption of spiritual energy / techniques | Subject to combat-doc limits |
| Rhythm-based interference | Timing attacks |
| Coordination / harmonisation | Support roles |
| Emotional or perceptual influence | **Limited** — not universal mind control |
| Silence, timing, subtlety | High-skill expression |

**Do not** assume every user gets soul attacks, automatic mind control, or emotion hacks.

---

## Provisional / illustrative — schools

Different traditions may emphasise resonance, layered rhythm, harmony, or **sound vs silence**. **No school names or formal subdivisions are canon** yet. Schools need **not** be separate Great Daos; walkers may blend or diverge.

---

## Established — relationship to sword cultivation (analogy)

Mirror the **Sword Dao / sword intent / physical sword** relationship ([`celestial-sword-sect.md`](celestial-sword-sect.md), [`blade-great-dao.md`](blade-great-dao.md)):

| Layer | Lean |
|-------|------|
| **Instrument** | Zither remains a **valuable, often superior** medium for precision, complexity, and power at high comprehension |
| **High realm** | May express comprehension **without** physically playing — but **do not** obsolete the instrument at peak |
| **Progression** | **No** mandatory “Zither Intent” ladder or new realm gates invented in this pass |
