## Intent

**囿道** *Yòu Dào* — **Enclosure Great Dao** — path whose Manifestation **defines a bounded place** where **one rule** holds harder than outside. Distinct from GC **Domain** ([`domain-system.md`](domain-system.md)) — **law legislation** at DM, not aura pressure alone.

## Rule sentence (Ge’s wear)

*Inside my fence, **first blood** is forbidden; the boundary **remembers**.*

## Who walks it

- Hermits, ward-makers, neutral-ground hosts — **Ge Hongli** [`worldly-law-bearer-ge-hongli.md`](worldly-law-bearer-ge-hongli.md)
