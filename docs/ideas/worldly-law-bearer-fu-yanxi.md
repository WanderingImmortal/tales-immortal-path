## One line

**Peak Dao Manifestation** — **傅演戏** *Fù Yǎnxì* — wears **Open Outcome** (**未结局大道**): *what has been decided is not closed*; child face, ancient voice, **戏坟老魔** *Play-Grave Old Demon*; **cheerful war protocol** and **props you can investigate**; **Law-weight** wanderer, not a broken clerk.

---

## Vibe (what he feels like)

**Not** horror-clown, **not** tragic PTSD soldier, **not** meta playwright.

**Yes:** a **small child** who **hosts a war that never adjourned** — utterly polite, utterly certain, **no hurry**. People feel **rushed** around *him*, not the other way around. You meet him on the road and your last **win** suddenly feels **premature**; your old **grudge** twitches; the corpse you stepped over feels **watched**.

| Gu | Pei | **Fu** |
|----|-----|--------|
| Gentle **after** horror | Games **hide** presence | **Ceremony** where **nothing ends** |
| Decay **settles** | Traceless **slips** | Open outcome **won’t let go** |
| You relax | You check your pouch | You check what you thought was **finished** |

**Jianghu fear:** “**戏坟** passed through” — don’t **celebrate victory** on that stretch; don’t **sign truces** you can’t afford to **reopen**.

**Tone lock:** investigative curiosity + unease — *why does he bow to **that** body?* — not slapstick.

## What his power **looks** like

### Rule (one sentence)

*What has been **decided** is not **closed**.* (**未结局大道** *Open Outcome Great Dao* — see [`open-outcome-great-dao.md`](open-outcome-great-dao.md))

### Embodied field (fiction)

When he **Manifests**, reality near him **won’t tie off**:

- **Ink** on contracts / merit lists stays **wet**; seals **won’t set**.
- **Qi knots** that should **lock** after a blow stay **half-tight** — like a bell **struck but not rung out**.
- **Sounds** carry a beat **too long** — laughter, challenge, **“done!”** — then **hang**.
- **Not** mass resurrection: corpses stay corpses; he treats them as **still in the bout** (salute, stake, **pre-victory** toast).

**Visual anchor:** child in **mess-officer** rags, cart **洪炉台** *Hónglú Tái* (misnamed); **empty birdcage**; cups chipped **胜** *shèng*; lists with **one wrong name** — all **clues**, not the magic itself.

### Out of combat (world)

| Symptom | Read |
|---------|------|
| Recent **quest closed** | Chronicle / NPC: “**unsettled**” chip — one thread **still tappable** |
| **Truce / debt** nearby | Situation threads **weighted** to **reopen** |
| He **mis-names** you | **Mis-seating** — you’re addressed as **rank you don’t hold** (or “**extra**” when you’re known) |
| Ignore him | Insult poem, wrong name on list, **walks on** — no chase |

### In combat (F3 target — [`dao-combat-target-spine.md`](dao-combat-target-spine.md))

**Domain legislation** inside volume:

1. **No clean finish** — lethal threshold / surrender / disengage needs **extra beat** or **contest** vs his depth+fill (“**has this fight **closed**?**”).
2. **Buffs don’t lock** — short **renewal** flicker (Circulation sector lean); **Consume**-like denial of “**settled**” regen states.
3. **Peel** weak — layers **stick** until you **leave** or win contest.
4. **He** fights like **closing is optional** — not highest burst; **drags** until you break field or **close** something with cost.

**Counters:** **leave volume**; **beasts**; fight with **no open threads**; future **Stillness** / **Severance**; drain **fill**.

## What it **plays** like

### Road encounter (likely first ship)

1. **Meet** — tea in **胜** cup (refuse = fine); he **slots** you on a list.
2. **Investigate** props → regiment / siege / **wrong name** line.
3. **Optional:** pay **teeth stake** (small coin) = accept you’re **in an open bet** with fate on that road — boon or **unsettled** scar.
4. **Optional:** fix list line → he **bows**, cart rolls away; chronicle clue.
5. **Avoid** if you just closed a big arc and want peace — **same as smart jianghu**.

**Play feel:** detective beats + **one social gamble**; not a forced cutscene play.

### If you fight him (rare, boss-tier)

**Feels like:** weather you **can’t dismiss** — you’re winning on HP but the **fight won’t end**; your **big finisher** needs **two steps**; escaping **outside** feels like **finally exhaling**. Allies want him **contested down** or **kited out** — not “burst the clown.”

**Not:** random status roulette; every tick ties to **closure**.

### Party composition fantasy

- **Gu** nearby: decay **settles** corpses; Fu **salutes** them anyway — odd couple, no required friendship.
- **Pei** steals cup; Fu calls “**brother thief**” — theft **doesn’t close** ownership in his head.
