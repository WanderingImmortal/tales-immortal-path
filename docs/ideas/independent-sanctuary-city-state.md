## Intent

An **independent city-state** founded early in the **Dao Wars** as a refuge, now (~3,000 years into the Imperial Sky Era) a **prosperous sanctuary and commercial hub** defended by layered formations and **four resident Dao Manifestation cultivators**. It is **not** a great sect, imperial dependency, or expansionist power.

**Naming:** the city and its principals have **no locked names** in this pass — use role labels in design until the owner assigns them.

**Label legend:** **Established (canon)** = agreed for downstream docs. **Provisional / preferred** = direction, not fixed detail. **Open / TBD** = do not invent without owner lock.

## Established — sanctuary principle

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Promise** | Within city jurisdiction and lawful behaviour, **outside factions** cannot arbitrarily seize, punish, assassinate, or abduct residents |
| **Refuge** | Pursued cultivators may **seek sanctuary**; old grievances **do not** auto-grant enforcement rights inside the walls |
| **Geographic limit** | **No** protection for those who leave jurisdiction, hunt abroad, or project force worldwide |
| **Law applies** | Sanctuary ≠ crime immunity — **city law** binds all realms, sects, wealth levels |
| **Credibility** | Institutions must show the powerful **cannot prey** on the weak with impunity |

## Established — law and governance (principles)

- Considerable **personal and commercial freedom**; **firm** enforcement.
- Laws aim to be **neither absurd nor arbitrary**.
- Serious threats: murder, coercion, kidnapping, **unauthorised extradition**, assassination, attacks on residents, sabotage of **defensive infrastructure**.
- **Mature institutions:** guards, investigators, legal authorities — the four Manifestation figures **do not** adjudicate every dispute.
- **Founder** = recognised **city lord** and principal authority; **delegates** extensively.
- **Other three** = influential **founding / senior companions** — shared trust, **not** necessarily equal co-rulers or ministers.
- **No manufactured** criminality or betrayal among the four for drama.

**Open / TBD:** formal constitution, titles, penalties, extradition procedures, credible accusations for crimes committed **before** arrival, jurisdiction over dependent villages, relationship to **imperial law**.

**Preserve:** protection from **outside** enforcement ≠ immunity from **accountability** (details later).

## Established — political and economic identity

- **Sanctuary + commerce** reinforce each other: security → trade confidence → wealth → formations and institutions → refuge credibility.
- Merchants value **predictable rules**, **independence**, access to **multiple factions** without sect allegiance.
- Refugees may treat it as near-**holy** safety; it is **not** a utopia — crime, inequality, disputes, corruption **may** exist.
- Distinction: **credible institutions** and shelter from **arbitrary external power**.

---

## Established — character and worldbuilding principles

- Powerful cultivators = **individuals** (earnest, reclusive, social, obsessive — mixed).
- **Not** every apex figure is secretly malicious or trauma-locked.
- **Earnest ≠ naive.** **Expertise + preparation** matter (especially formations).
- Bandits and assassins may be **high-realm** and competent.
- Prepared formation masters are **hard to dislodge**; Manifestation experts are **not** omnipotent.
- Survival = **incentives**, not plot armour.
- Long-lived companions can build **real friendships** and **lasting institutions**.
- City remains part of a **reactive world**, not a static player mall.

Aligns with [`imperial-clan.md`](imperial-clan.md) world-tone principle where applicable.
