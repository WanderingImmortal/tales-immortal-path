## Intent

The steps from Peak Dao Manifestation to each kind of qi Immortal, and where World Fortune is touched along the way. Refining World Fortune and ascending are the same process seen from two sides, so they are sketched together. Qi path only. Body (Demi-Saint → Saint) and soul (Pseudo God → God) come later and may look different.

Goal: every step should follow from rules already in the docs, not from new parts.

## The three qi Immortals

From the borrowed / stolen / self-made table in [`heaven-cycle-and-apexes.md`](heaven-cycle-and-apexes.md):

| Kind | Source | Cosmology name | Trial family after ascending |
|------|--------|----------------|------------------------------|
| **Mandate Immortal** | Borrowed world fortune | Registry Immortal, Door A | Immortal Calamity (audit) |
| **Heavenly Demon** | Stolen — devoured from others | (no row yet) | Open |
| **True Immortal** | Self-made | Door B, or converted upstairs | Dao Trial / Self Tribulation |

Chaos Ascendants are outside this table. They leave heaven's taxonomy entirely ([`chaos-cultivation-path.md`](chaos-cultivation-path.md)).

**Suggestion — one line that separates them:** all three take hold of World Fortune at the end. They differ in **who refines it, and whether they keep it.**

| Kind | Who refines the fortune | Keeps it? |
|------|-------------------------|-----------|
| Mandate | Heaven, on your behalf | Yes — as a loan on the ledger |
| Heavenly Demon | You, from fortune already inside other beings | Yes — but it never settles |
| True | You, against your own Dao | No — released at the end |

## Stage 1 — Peak Dao Manifestation

Already designed in [`dao-seeking-and-manifestation.md`](dao-seeking-and-manifestation.md): one worn law, depth Wielded → Refined → Imposed → Peak. Ordinary peak. The lifespan clock (≈45–50k from first wield) is running and does not stop.

Nothing here touches World Fortune. A Peak Manifestation cultivator imposes a law on the world's *produce* — its qi, its matter — not on the stock under it.

## Stage 2 — Half-Step Immortal

Already defined in [`nine-realm-ladder.md`](nine-realm-ladder.md): a second peak, not a realm. A limited claim on the next realm's abilities, permitted because the life still ends.

**Suggestion: what the claim *is*.** The Half-Step is the stage where you first reach the stock. The "limited claim" is a limited hold on World Fortune.

**Suggestion: why heaven permits it.** Anything a Half-Step holds goes back to the world when they die, and they will die — the clock is still running. Heaven lets a mortal hold a loan it is certain to collect. That is the existing rule ("permitted because the life still ends") given a reason. It needs no new part.

### How you get there (premise reworked 2026-10-02 — under owner review)

**Retired:** "the end of a law is World Fortune." A law is a rule. World Fortune is a reserve. A rule does not end in a reserve, so that line mixed two kinds of thing and would have become a plot hole.

**Replacement — laws say *how*, fortune is *what with*.**

A law is a rule in heaven's rulebook: how fire burns, how qi gathers, how a cut separates. Every time a law runs, it spends something: qi moves, heat is given off, matter changes. What it spends is the world's **produce**. The produce rises from the **stock**. That matches the existing rule that everything in the world is on loan from the cycle ([`heaven-cycle-and-apexes.md`](heaven-cycle-and-apexes.md)). The rule is the *how*. Fortune is what pays for it.

Running a law spends produce, and produce renews from the stock, like a river fed by a lake. Only drawing on the stock itself thins the world. Manifestation cultivators do not drain World Fortune by fighting.

**Each qi realm takes one more step into the rulebook:**

| Realm | Relation to heaven's rules |
|-------|----------------------------|
| Peak Void Refinement | **Glimpses** the rules moving, from outside. Trespass, so heaven retaliates. |
| Dao Seeking | **Reads** them. The library. |
| Dao Manifestation | **Runs** one rule by their own will, in their own space. |
| Half-Step | **Sees what the rules run on.** |
| Immortal | **Draws on it.** |

**Why Peak, and not earlier.** The existing depth names already describe heaven stepping back:

- *Wielded:* a local skew on how heaven runs the law.
- *Refined:* reliable imposition.
- *Imposed:* space answers, and heaven reads your law.
- *Peak:* within your space, you run the law as completely as heaven does.

Below Peak, heaven is still doing most of the running, and you are leaning on its work. At Peak you are doing heaven's job in that space. From heaven's side of a rule, you can see what it is paid with. That is not the law's floor. It is heaven's seat, and the stock is visible from it.

**Why reading is not enough.** Seeking tells you how fire behaves. Only running fire tells you what it costs. A Seeker with a vast library never sees the stock.

**Your intuition, made specific.** "In tune with the world enough to sense it" is the right feel. The rule above says why it is the stock and not just world qi: Peak Manifesters already sense world qi, which is produce. What is new is seeing where the produce comes from.

**Is it all laws? Yes.**

- Every law runs on the same reserve, so any law worn to Peak gets there.
- That keeps the Lesser-law lock ("a valid endgame identity"). A Light Sword specialist can become a Half-Step.
- Greater and Lesser differ in **what you see through**, not *whether* you see:
  - A Greater law runs across a wide span of the world, so it shows the stock widely and shallowly.
  - A Lesser law is a narrow channel, so it shows a thin slice clearly.
- The worn law also flavours the sense. A Fire Half-Step feels the stock as warmth under volcanic veins. A Sword Half-Step feels it as the places where things are held apart. This gives a gameplay difference: which sacred grounds and windows you notice first.

**Depth and breadth** (the open question in the Manifestation doc):

- **Depth gets you there.** You need one rule run completely.
- **Breadth decides how much you can hold.** Your worn law lets you see the stock. Gripping it needs knowing how *other* rules draw on it. Each comprehended law in your library is another handhold. A narrow cultivator sees the stock clearly but holds little.

**Consequences that fall out:**

- **Swap your worn law and you lose the seat.** You are back to the rule heaven runs, until the new law reaches Peak. That is the first of the falling-back routes below.
- **Hook (not locked):** a Karma Dao Half-Step sees the stock *and* the ties around it. The bridge rule (nameless karma becomes fortune) would be visible to them happening.
- **Body and soul paths** reach their apex stock by other routes. Body Dao is an oath law worn in the flesh, not in space. Leave them for their own pass.

### Three steps inside the Half-Step (sketch)

These are not sub-realms. They are what a Half-Step learns, in order.

| Step | What you can do | What it gives |
|------|-----------------|---------------|
| **Sense** | See World Fortune. Sacred grounds glow. You feel where waves landed. Living Immortals show as dams. | Finding windows and corpses before others. Recognising other Immortals for what they are. |
| **Grip** | Take hold of a thread of the stock for a short time, then let go. | The limited claim: brief immortal-grade feats fuelled by the borrowed thread. Holding longer or more draws notice and backlash. |
| **Refine** | Practise refining a thread into your own body and Dao, under strain. | Readiness for the draw. How much you can refine is how much you will draw at Ascension. |

**This sizes the draw** (an open question in [`personal-fortune.md`](personal-fortune.md)): Ascension draws what you have learned to grip and refine. Breadth and Half-Step practice set it. A thin world gives less to grip, so everyone ascends smaller in a thin age.

**Corpses fit here.** A sacred ground is fortune already pooled and already refined once. That makes it the easiest place for a Half-Step to practise gripping, and a common place to ascend from. The owner hook "half-steps fuel ascension from a corpse" becomes the normal case for anyone who can find one.

**A Tianjing hook (not locked).** The Tian founder was the first public Half-Step, and in the walk he hunted the Heavenly Demon's army first. A Half-Step can *sense* stolen fortune — it doesn't settle, so it looks wrong. He may have seen what they were before anyone told him.

### Falling back

Open in the ladder doc. **Suggestion:** yes, two ways.

- **Swap or break the worn law.** Depth got you there. Lose the depth and you lose the floor.
- **Hold too much for too long.** A mortal who keeps the loan past what death would return is no longer a safe loan. Heaven stops permitting the claim. Backlash drops you to ordinary peak.

## Stage 3 — Ascension and the fork

The Heavenly Tribulation for Immortal Ascension. You draw what you can refine, under the lightning. At the end there is one moment that decides the kind.

### Mandate Immortal — let heaven finish it (Door A)

You draw the share. At the end of the tribulation heaven offers to finish the refining for you: recognition, the stamp, the registry entry. Accept, and heaven refines the share into your immortal body and keeps the books on it.

- **This reconciles Rule 7 with "the ascender draws."** You reach and grip; heaven refines and records. The draw is yours. The refined form is heaven's. That is exactly what "borrowed" means.
- **Why Immortal Calamities exist:** heaven holds the refined form, so it audits it. A share that costs more than it is worth gets culled.
- **Why it is the easy door:** the hardest refining, the last stage, is done for you.
- **Departure:** a borrowed share cannot cross the gate. To leave, a Mandate Immortal must first convert — refine the share themselves and release it. That is the Dao Trial upstairs (cosmology model 3).

### True Immortal — finish it yourself and let go (Door B)

You draw the same share. You refuse the stamp. You refine yourself *against* the share — use it as the mould your immortal Dao sets in — and then release it.

- **Self-made:** what holds you up afterwards is your own Dao, not the share. The cosmology's "no lease, no ∞ yet" fits: you gave up the easy sustenance and must hold yourself.
- **The share returns at once.** A True ascension is followed by a small fortune wave where it happened. That is a tell in the world: an ascension with no new dam, and a window opening nearby.
- **Matches the earlier lean:** Door B "never drained anything." It drew, and gave it all back.
- **Why it is the hard door:** you must do the final refining yourself, in the lightning, and then let go of the thing keeping you alive.
- **Why it is rare:** most never learn there is a choice. The method is the kind that left with the people who knew it.

**Conversion later is the same act, postponed.** A Mandate Immortal who converts upstairs is doing what a Door B ascender did at the gate. So there is one True Immortal, reached at the gate or after it.

### Heavenly Demon — take it from those who already hold it

The Demon does not draw from the open stock. Everything alive holds a little of the world on loan, and death repays it. The Demon refines what others hold — qi, blood, soul — before it is repaid.

- **The world still thins, without any draw.** Repayments never arrive. Heaven sees no draw on the stock at Ascension; the books just stop balancing.
- **Stolen fortune never settles.** It was refined out of someone else's shape, so it keeps trying to return. The Demon must keep feeding to hold it. That is the hunger, and it is why he devours tribute and failed merit for ages.
- **He cannot leave.** Nothing stolen crosses the gate. The devourer is the permanent dam in the departure table.
- **Open:** does he face the Heavenly Tribulation at all? Options: (a) yes, and he passed it like anyone; (b) he never ascended through it, and is an Immortal by mass, not by the gate; (c) heaven's counter-rule is the never-settling itself, not a tribulation.

### What this explains

- **Why today's Immortals don't leave** (forced question 2 in [`personal-fortune.md`](personal-fortune.md)). Almost all are Mandate. Departure needs conversion, conversion is the hardest trial, and the people who knew how left. Both of the owner's answers — "very hard" and "method lost" — fall out of one rule.
- **Why the age thins.** Mandate is the easy door. Easy doors are taken. Each one is a dam.
- **Why Door B is hidden.** Heaven doesn't hide it. It just never offers it. Heaven offers the stamp; the refusal is something you must already know about.
