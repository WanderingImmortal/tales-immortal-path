## Two beats — tide, then proof

| Beat | What happened | Who understood |
|------|----------------|----------------|
| **1 — The tide** | The **Tian founder** broke through to **Half-Step Immortal** *during* the siege — first soul on the continent to touch the gate. **Every** major camp felt the realm shift: arrays flickered, oaths tightened, old monsters woke. | Sages, array masters, peak elders — *something* ended. **Not yet** who won. |
| **2 — The garden** | The breakthrough **was not decisive** until the Half-Step **stepped onto the field** at the coalition’s grandest muster — the **Azure Gate** (working name: 天门关 / proto-capital ramparts). Power **no one had wielded before**. Armies and grand formations parted like weeds. | **Horror** — but **denial** lasted weeks. Pride, propaganda, and “maybe it was a trick” delayed the peace. |

**Design line:** *Militarily swift, politically messy.* One walk ended the coalition **as a fighting force**; **surrenders, purges, and charter** took seasons to years.

## The coalition assault

Rumors had run for years: the Tian clan bred a **genius** assailing heights no house should reach. **Spies sat in every camp** — Lotus marriage lines, Sword honor guards, Phoenix flame envoys, demon blood-seers, Void archivists copying dispatches.

The **grand coalition** gathered to **snuff the Tian** before the genius became the board:

- **Golden Phoenix** — largest martial column; Flame Emperor pushing **Realm Reforge** (#8); pride on the line.
- **Lesser clans & noble houses** — betting everything on one throw; many would **never recover**.
- **Heavenly Demon Cult** — **not** there to crown a king; there to **harvest** the siege (cauldrons behind the coalition, tagged formations on both sides). Coalition command **tolerated** them until they didn’t.
- **Celestial Sword** — contributed heirs and licensed duels; Sword Immortal **did not** lead the assault *(Dao Manifestation, still below the new ceiling)*.
- **Jade Lotus** — **not** in the assault column *(see below)*.
- **Void Temple** — **refused** to march; seal priority over charter.

**Tian defenses held one beat too long** — long enough for the genius to **finish** the breakthrough **inside** the cordon. That delay is why Tian myth calls it **the dragon’s patience**; coalition myth calls it **the curse of overconfidence**.

## The garden walk — what everyone saw

The Half-Step emerged from the inner keep / array core and **walked outward**. Grand formations **failed to constrain**. Coalition battle-lines **broke without a protracted duel** — not because enemies lacked courage, but because **the category of force had changed**.

| Targeted with particular ferocity | Why (Tian / orthodox memory) |
|-----------------------------------|------------------------------|
| **Heavenly Demon army** (cauldron legions, harvest arrays) | Worst cycle-tamperers; **fed on the siege**; Half-Step **hunted them first** in the walk — public proof the mandate was not “another sect” |
| **Demon-linked formations** | Blood pools that tried to **tag** the Half-Step’s wake — unmade on sight |

Cult inner court: the walk **shredded** the field army; survivors ordered **Great Withdrawal** ([`heavenly-demon-cult.md`](heavenly-demon-cult.md)). Mandate later claims **annihilation** at this battle; juniors think it’s folklore.

## Aftershocks — pride, bloom, recede, ruin

| Power | Immediate reaction | Settled truth |
|-------|-------------------|---------------|
| **Golden Phoenix** | **Would not surrender** while ember still hot — Flame Emperor withdrew **burning**, not kneeling. Pride **forbade** public acceptance for a season. | **Late** capitulation after paragon **dies** (follow-up war / Realm Reforge push — see [`golden-phoenix-sect.md`](golden-phoenix-sect.md)). Younger brother eventually kneels to save caldera. |
| **Celestial Sword** | Saw the shift **before** pride could spend the Sword Immortal. **Receded** — **Ten Thousand Swords Seal Array**, envoys, **no** suicide duel. | **First great sect** to accept charter terms **after** the Capital Turn — still taught as “first to kneel,” humiliating but **mountain intact**. |
| **Jade Lotus** | **Bloomed** — public grain, marriage reaffirmation, charter lawyers **for the throne**. Textbooks: *“Lotus backed the winner.”* | **Had hedged for years** (supply, bonds, spies). **Did not** assault the capital; **profited** from coalition’s collapse. Sword calls it opportunism; Lotus calls it **roots**. |
| **Void Temple** | Felt tide; **stayed** on prison duty. Signed **Archive Oath** once outcome was clear. | Neutral **late**, not weak. |
| **Lesser clans** | Trembled; many **already bled** dry joining the coalition. | **Ruin wave** — names lost; Heartlands four are what **survived** with homelands. |
| **Heavenly Demon Cult** | Field army **broken**; patriarch orders **Withdrawal**. | Dormant shadow; **extirpation** fiction born here. |

**Psychology (owner beat):** horror of the garden walk **was not fully accepted straight away** — coalition survivors, Phoenix pamphlets, and demon propaganda all pushed “**trick array**,” “**borrowed power**,” “**he’ll collapse tomorrow**” until charter oaths made denial expensive.

## Lore tensions — what this changes (and how we fit)

| Existing line | Tension | Resolution (v1) |
|---------------|---------|-----------------|
| Imperial: wars ended **“swiftly”** | vs delayed **acceptance** | **Swift** = coalition **military** break at Azure Gate; **slow** = Phoenix refusal, negotiations, lesser-clan collapse |
| Sword: **“early capitulation”** | vs recede **at** the turn | **Early** = first among **charter great sects** **after** the garden walk — **not** early in the multi-decade war |
| Lotus: **“early alliance”** / valley retreat | vs **“backed the winner”** at capital | **Two timelines:** mid-war **squeeze + valley consolidation**; **late-war** covert Tian ties; **post-walk** public **bloom** — “early ally” means **early to the mandate**, not early to the siege |
| Phoenix: Flame Emperor’s death **“ended the war”** | vs Half-Step **already** broke the board | Garden walk **ends the coalition**; **Phoenix war** continues until paragon dies **refusing** kneel — **brother’s kneel** ends **open** war |
| Void: “while Sword capitulated, Lotus allied, Phoenix broke” | order of events | All **after** Capital Turn; Void **never** marched |
| Demon: withdrawal from **fear** of Half-Step | vs **targeted** on field | **Both** — broken in the walk **then** strategic dormancy; scripture still says **wise**, not coward |
| Sword Immortal “not enough to stop breakthrough” | could he have been at coalition? | **No duel to death** — read tide, **recede**; ascends **after** mandate |
