## Two beats — tide, then proof

| Beat | What happened | Who understood |
|------|----------------|----------------|
| **1 — The tide** | The **Tian founder** broke through to **Half-Step Immortal** *during* the siege — first soul on the continent to touch the gate. **Every** major camp felt the realm shift: arrays flickered, oaths tightened, old monsters woke. | Sages, array masters, peak elders — *something* ended. **Not yet** who won. |
| **2 — The garden** | The breakthrough **was not decisive** until the Half-Step **stepped onto the field** before **Tianjing’s ancient gates** — the Tian clan’s own city in the Dao Wars ([`imperial-city-tianjing.md`](imperial-city-tianjing.md)). Inner ramparts and **deep arrays** had held the coalition **one beat too long**; then power **no one had wielded before** walked outward. Armies and grand formations parted like weeds. | **Horror** — etched into every survivor who was there. Who **admits** it aloud differs by sect. |

**Design line:** *Militarily swift, politically messy.* One walk ended the coalition **as a fighting force**; **surrenders, purges, and charter** took seasons to years.

## The siege of Tianjing (天京)

**Tianjing** was already the **Tian clan’s city** — not yet wrapped in today’s **Longcheng** outer sprawl. The imperial metropolis **grew outward after** the mandate; charter Longcheng is layers of hub, sect branches, and noble quarters built on victory’s scaffolding. What the coalition assaulted was the **inner seat**: ancient walls, underpalace veins, and defenses **older and deeper** than anything the four sects openly admit to.

Rumors had run for years: the Tian bred a **genius** at **peak Dao Manifestation**, assailing Half-Step. **Spies** sat in every camp — but the only **great powers** who actually marched to **snuff the Tian** at the capital were:

| Belligerent | Role |
|-------------|------|
| **Celestial Sword** | Full martial commitment — **Sword Ancestor** (剑祖) era: the one who would become **Sword Immortal** (剑仙), **peak Dao Manifestation**, **wearing the law of the First Sword**. A **real peer threat** to the Tian founder **before** the breakthrough — **not** an Immortal yet. |
| **Golden Phoenix** | Largest flame column; **Flame Emperor** pushing **Realm Reforge** (#8). |
| **Heavenly Demon Cult** | **Harvest** on the siege — cauldrons, tagged formations; tolerated by coalition until the walk. |
| **Lesser clans & houses** | Many joined; most **ruined** — *parked: named survivors for later content* |

**Did not fight the Tian at the capital:**

| Power | Stance |
|-------|--------|
| **Jade Lotus** | **Would not** war against the Tian — squeezed by Phoenix/Sword elsewhere, consolidated valley, **supply and marriage hedges** toward the rising clan; **bloomed** after the walk ([`jade-lotus-sect.md`](jade-lotus-sect.md)). |
| **Void Temple** | **Silent** for most of the war — prison and seals first; **Archive Oath** only after outcome clear ([`void-temple-sect.md`](void-temple-sect.md)). |

**The two apexes (pre-walk):** Tian founder and **Sword Ancestor** both **peak Dao Manifestation** — the board’s ceiling **before** Half-Step existed. Chroniclers still argue whether the Sword Ancestor could have **killed** the genius **during** breakthrough if the inner defenses had failed one hour sooner.

**Tian defenses held one beat too long** — long enough for the genius to **finish** Half-Step **inside** Tianjing’s cordon. Tian myth: **dragon’s patience**. Coalition myth: **curse of overconfidence**.

## The garden walk — what everyone saw

The Half-Step emerged from Tianjing’s **inner keep / underpalace core** and **walked outward** through the gates. Grand formations **failed to constrain**. Sword and Phoenix battle-lines **broke** — not for lack of courage, but because **the category of force had changed**.

**Flame Emperor** **saw** the destruction at the gates. He **tested** the Half-Step’s power — a **brief, terrible exchange** — and **withdrew** rather than kneel, **injured** but alive. Phoenix inner court **does not deny** this; shame and pride **forbid** softening it into propaganda.

**Sword Ancestor** read the tide **before** the mountain spent him. He **did not** duel the Half-Step to annihilation. He **receded** to Solitary Sword Mountain, raised the **Ten Thousand Swords Seal Array**, and sent envoys. The sect **openly accepts** their loss and their part in the assault — then **returns to the sword** ([`celestial-sword-sect.md`](celestial-sword-sect.md)).

| Targeted with particular ferocity | Why (Tian / orthodox memory) |
|-----------------------------------|------------------------------|
| **Heavenly Demon army** (cauldron legions, harvest arrays) | Worst cycle-tamperers; **fed on the siege**; Half-Step **hunted them first** in the walk — public proof the mandate was not “another sect” |
| **Demon-linked formations** | Blood pools that tried to **tag** the Half-Step’s wake — unmade on sight |

Cult inner court: the walk **shredded** the field army; survivors ordered **Great Withdrawal** ([`heavenly-demon-cult.md`](heavenly-demon-cult.md)). Mandate later claims **annihilation** at this battle; juniors think it’s folklore.

## Aftershocks — pride, bloom, recede, ruin

| Power | Memory culture | Settled truth |
|-------|----------------|---------------|
| **Golden Phoenix** | **Pride forbids denial** — the walk and the Flame Emperor’s **injury** are **recorded**, not mythologized away. Shame is carried **openly** in inner court; outer pamphlets are about **future** renewal, not “it was a trick.” | **Late** open war after retreat until paragon **dies**; younger brother kneels to save caldera ([`golden-phoenix-sect.md`](golden-phoenix-sect.md)). |
| **Celestial Sword** | **No shame in honesty** — they **lost**, they **marched on Tianjing**, they **survived** with the mountain. Politics bore them; they **seek the sword**. | First charter capitulation **after** the walk; Sword Ancestor ascends **Immortal** only **after** mandate settles. |
| **Jade Lotus** | **Never** assaulted Tianjing; **bloomed** into best charter terms once outcome was clear. | Hedged supply and bonds; **not** a belligerent — Sword still calls it opportunism. |
| **Void Temple** | **Silence** — most of the war unremarked; oath **after** the fact. | Prison held; gates stayed closed. |
| **Lesser clans** | Many **broken** on the march; a few names may still linger *(content later)*. | Ruin wave; Heartlands “four” are what survived with homelands + mandate. |
| **Heavenly Demon Cult** | Field army **broken**; **Withdrawal**; juniors forget. | Extirpation fiction born at Tianjing gates. |

**Psychology (owner beat):** lesser houses and mortal armies **whispered** trick arrays for a season; **Phoenix and Sword cultures differ** — Phoenix **owns** the shame without denying facts; Sword **owns** the loss and **leaves** politics to Longcheng.

## Lore tensions — what this changes (and how we fit)

| Existing line | Tension | Resolution (v1) |
|---------------|---------|-----------------|
| Imperial: wars ended **“swiftly”** | vs messy peace | **Swift** = military break at **Tianjing gates**; **slow** = Phoenix follow-up war, lesser-clan ruin |
| Sword: **“early capitulation”** | vs recede **at** the turn | **Early** = first among **charter great sects** **after** the garden walk — **not** early in the multi-decade war |
| Lotus: **“early alliance”** / valley retreat | vs **“backed the winner”** at capital | **Two timelines:** mid-war **squeeze + valley consolidation**; **late-war** covert Tian ties; **post-walk** public **bloom** — “early ally” means **early to the mandate**, not early to the siege |
| Phoenix: Flame Emperor’s death **“ended the war”** | vs Half-Step **already** broke the board | Garden walk **ends the coalition**; **Phoenix war** continues until paragon dies **refusing** kneel — **brother’s kneel** ends **open** war |
| Void: “while Sword capitulated, Lotus allied, Phoenix broke” | order of events | All **after** Capital Turn; Void **never** marched |
| Demon: withdrawal from **fear** of Half-Step | vs **targeted** on field | **Both** — broken in the walk **then** strategic dormancy; scripture still says **wise**, not coward |
| Sword Immortal **not ascended** in war | peer threat | War-era **Sword Ancestor** (剑祖) = **peak DM**; **剑仙** only **after** peace |
| Phoenix denial vs pride | old “denial season” | Phoenix **records** the walk; shame yes, **deny** no |
| Lotus at capital | assault column | **Never** fought Tian; hedged **elsewhere** |
| **Tianjing** vs Longcheng | single capital name | **Tianjing** = Dao Wars Tian city + today’s **inner** ring; **Longcheng** = post-mandate **outer** expansion |
