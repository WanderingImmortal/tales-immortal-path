## Intent

Give the **Tian Clan** a human family spine: a stern father shaped by war, a mother lost early, and two brothers whose complementary achievements founded the imperial dynasty. The clan should feel like **people shaped by circumstances**, not a stack of legendary archetypes.

**Authoritative companions:** [`imperial-clan.md`](imperial-clan.md) (mandate & power model), [`imperial-city-tianjing.md`](imperial-city-tianjing.md) (capital & court), [`dao-wars-capital-turn.md`](dao-wars-capital-turn.md) (siege & garden walk).

**This document supersedes** the older owner-locked arrangement that made **Tian Xu** the reigning emperor (young VR-peak face of the mandate) and treated the founding Immortal as literally asleep / unconscious beneath Tianjing. See § Superseded claims.

```text
[ Warring States Era ]
    Tian Clan already a formidable ancient cultivation clan
    Previous patriarch leads through late Warring States → Dao Wars
    Mother dies comparatively early (cause Open)
        ↓
[ Dao Wars — shortly before the siege of Tianjing ]
    Previous patriarch assassinated (killer / client / motive Open)

        (little or no proper succession ceremony)

        ↓

    Elder brother remains the reigning emperor
```

## The previous patriarch (father)

| Layer | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Canon** | Tian Clan patriarch in the later Warring States Era and Dao Wars. Stern, demanding, experienced leader shaped by prolonged conflict. Capable of ruthlessness while sincerely wishing for peace. Assassinated shortly before the siege of Tianjing. |
| **Provisional** | Harshness was substantially a product of responsibility and a dangerous world — not lack of affection. Sons remember him for **values, discipline, and lessons**, not frequent tenderness. |
| **Canon — private hope** | He privately wished that someone might eventually unify the continent and end the endless wars. He did **not** necessarily expect to do it himself, and he did **not** leave a secret imperial master plan. |
| **Must not become** | A prophecy, secret scheme, or explicit instruction to found an empire. |

His private hope for peace is an important character trait. It colours how the brothers remember him; it does not author the dynasty.

### Assassination — preserve uncertainty

Extraordinary accomplishment: credible preparation, opportunity, or specialised expertise.

**Do not establish (all Open):**

- Killer's identity, cultivation, organisation, or survival status
- Whether there was a paying client
- Commissioned vs opportunistic
- One unified Assassins Guild vs several independent organisations *as explanation for this killing*
- Whether any guild deliberately prolonged the war
- Whether the reigning emperor knows the assassin's identity or pursued revenge

The assassination is **not** the sole cause of the younger brother's breakthrough or the Dao Wars' conclusion.

*(Present-day Longcheng gray-layer Assassins Guild fiction in [`imperial-city-tianjing.md`](imperial-city-tianjing.md) is separate modern institutional design — do not retroactively bind it to this murder without a later owner lock.)*

## The elder brother — patriarch, then reigning emperor

**Temporary designation:** *the reigning Tian emperor (elder brother)*. Personal name **Open** — do not invent a canonical given name in other docs.

| Layer | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Canon** | Recognised heir; educated for leadership, diplomacy, military command, and clan administration. Inherited under emergency wartime conditions after the assassination. **Immediate** choice: duty and clan survival before vengeance — he did not need centuries to learn that lesson. Coordinated Tianjing's defence **alongside** elders, commanders, and formation specialists (not alone). After the younger brother's breakthrough, became the **principal political architect and governing sovereign** of the new Tian imperial dynasty. **Still reigns** ~3,000 years later. |
| **Provisional realm** | Powerful long-lived cultivator; **advanced Dao Manifestation expert**. Exact stage and any mapping to nine-realm numerical tier — reconcile later without inventing a final index. |
| **Relationship** | Genuine affection and mutual respect with his younger brother. Can visit the Immortal for personal conversation, counsel, or family matters — **not** a puppet-ruler / hidden-master arrangement. |

### Personality (canon direction)

- Values stability, continuity, and the long-term wellbeing of the realm
- Understands prolonged warfare firsthand
- Enjoys some comforts and privileges of imperial life
- Takes governing seriously without personally managing every local matter
- Pragmatic; capable of political ruthlessness; not automatically correct or benevolent in every decision
- Cares about subjects **collectively** without needing to know or personally protect every individual
- **Potential flaw:** can mistake threats to Tian authority for threats to peace (not a mandatory plot event)
- Father's lessons shaped him; **his decisions and achievements remain his own**

He inherited a clan under siege and subsequently helped create an empire.

## The younger brother — imperial founding Immortal

| Layer | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Canon** | Exceptional cultivation genius. Breakthrough to **Half-Step Immortal** decisively changed the Dao Wars. **Founding figure of the imperial dynasty**, **not** founder of the ancient Tian Clan. Clan strength, inheritance, ancient formations, and political importance **predate** his breakthrough. Entered seclusion with clan support to pursue an approaching unprecedented breakthrough — success not guaranteed. Breakthrough = cultivation and comprehension culmination, **not** revenge-powered response to the father's assassination. Preserve Capital Turn sequence (garden walk, Seven Days, Ember Crown, **Dragon's Edict** / 龙敕 working name). |
| **Present** | An **Immortal** who **voluntarily remains in seclusion** beneath Tianjing — actively cultivating, contemplating, researching, and refining. **Not** literally unconscious or asleep. Cosmological restrictions on Immortals still bind him; he does not automatically intervene in ordinary administration. |
| **Further cultivation** | Need not pursue a new realm; may deepen comprehension, refine techniques, or study other disciplines. |
| **Open** | True personal name (pre-mandate / private); exact post-ascension practice agenda. |

### "Sleeping dragon" language

| Use | Status |
|-----|--------|
| Public mythology, euphemism, court motto, continental metaphor | **Keep** |
| Literal unconsciousness / magical sleep / corpse-like burial | **Superseded** |

The underpalace remains a sealed, restricted deterrence site. "Wake the dragon" / "stir the seal" language can stay as crisis fiction for **intervention on the mortal board**, not for ending a coma.

## Complementary achievements

| Brother | What he did | What he did not do alone |
|---------|-------------|---------------------------|
| **Elder** | Emergency patriarchy; wartime coordination of Tianjing's defence; charter politics; empire's governing architecture; continuous reign | Did not personally supply the Half-Step ceiling that broke the coalition |
| **Younger** | Half-Step (then Immortal) watershed; garden walk; Seven Days; Ember Crown judgment / Dragon's Edict; founding Immortal deterrence | Did not invent the Tian Clan; did not single-handedly invent imperial administration |
| **Elder → younger (Ember Crown)** | Advised on settlement risk while Flame Emperor remained defiant | Did **not** order the execution — younger brother **chose** confrontation as continental signal (detail: [`dao-wars-capital-turn.md` — Ember Crown Plain](dao-wars-capital-turn.md#ember-crown-plain--character-politics-settlement-2026-11-07)) |

**Together:** one brother kept the clan alive and then ruled; the other changed what power *meant* on the continent and then stepped back into restrained seclusion. Family first; legend second.
