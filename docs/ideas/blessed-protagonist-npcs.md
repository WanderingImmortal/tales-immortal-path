## Intent

Some people in a xianxia world are not merely talented. Treasures surface where they happen to stand, a killing blow misses by a finger, and a desperate fight becomes the hour they comprehend the next realm. Readers recognize the shape immediately: the person the novel would have followed if the camera had not been on you.

That shape is worth having. The first draft of this doc called them **Blessed** and let heaven spend luck on a favorite. That premise does not survive this setting. Heaven is impartial rules, not a patron ([`heaven-cycle-and-apexes.md`](heaven-cycle-and-apexes.md)). It does not prefer a junior in Redwell to anyone else.

Working true name: **fortune knot** (气运结). A lump of circulating fortune has **caught** on a person. Folk still say "blessed," "heaven's darling," "child of fortune." That rumor is wrong in the same way elders are wrong when they blame every thin year on the Dao War. The pattern is real. The chooser is not.

They exist to press a specific player fantasy: *I am the prodigy, the one chances follow, the one a peer cannot quite finish.* Meet a knot, and that fantasy has a prior claimant. The sting is 山外有山, 天外有天 — a mountain behind the mountain. You were the genius of the well. A thicker deposit is already walking around somewhere else.

They are unbelievably hard to kill **among peers**, and they **can** die. The deposit buys scenes. When it is spent, a sword works. Death returns whatever is left.

Parents and neighbors: [`personal-fortune.md`](personal-fortune.md) (the balance these NPCs are a high reading of — increase, decrease, and wielding live there), [`heaven-cycle-and-apexes.md`](heaven-cycle-and-apexes.md) (heaven is rules, death repays loans), [`post-immortal-cosmology.md`](post-immortal-cosmology.md) (World Fortune — the age, not this balance), [`jianghu-situation-threads.md`](jianghu-situation-threads.md) (the grudge after you cut someone important), [`procedural-zone-sect-ecology.md`](procedural-zone-sect-ecology.md) (sect `prodigy` slot = talent, not this), [`redwell-starter-city.md`](redwell-starter-city.md) (NPC "chance to ascend" parked — this is the named, tiny version).

## Why fortune catches on them

**The balance, the postings, the burn, and the death-return are defined in [`personal-fortune.md`](personal-fortune.md).** This section only says how a *high* balance looks on an NPC. If the two docs disagree about increase, decrease, or wielding, personal fortune wins.

A knot is a tracked personal fortune, not a second currency and not a gift from a partial heaven. Circulation posted a lump onto this life. Impartial is not the same as even: a river floods one bank.

Three snags are enough. Any one can produce the same NPC. v1 only needs the chronicle to be able to hint at one of them. The integer does not care which.

| Snag | What happened | Why it looks like favor |
|------|----------------|-------------------------|
| **Natal posting** | A death, a broken vein, a sealed hoard, a parent who died with an open account. The cycle posts the balance onto the nearest open life — often a child born in that spot. | "Born under a lucky star." They inherited a deposit. |
| **Deed settlement** | One act the rules treat as a sudden large repayment or withdrawal: killing something that was hoarding fortune, cracking a seal, walking out of a tribulation that should have taken the deposit back. | "Heaven rewarded their daring." Morality is not in the formula. A butcher and a saint can both snag. |
| **Vacuum drain** | A place whose fortune has nowhere to go — ruined sect, stoppered vein, a sleeper's held pool leaking — dumps onto the most porous mortal nearby. Young, not yet knotted by oaths, sometimes a strange root. | "The sect's genius appeared from nowhere." They are a drainage ditch. |

The protagonist *effects* are how a lump unwinds through a living person:

- It completes events. A find, a patron, a breakthrough, a narrow survival. Those are drips back into the world, spent through a life instead of bursting on the ground.
- Keeping the vessel intact is one of those drips. Among peers, the lump can buy a single absurd escape so the rest of the deposit still has a life to unwind through. That is the "hard to kill." It is not protection of a favorite.
- When the lump is gone, the escapes stop. A two-realm gap is larger than the drip can pay, so the escape is refused. Death always returns the remainder at once.

Nobody is topping the deposit back up because they lived. A local knot of 3, spent once every few years, is a decade of uncanny luck and then an ordinary cultivator. A larger knot lasts longer because there is more to spend, not because heaven keeps choosing them. A new snag (another posting, another vacuum) can add fortune later. That is a new event, not a regen timer.

The player's **Heavenly Luck** trait is a different object: a small channel they paid for, breakthrough odds against thinner tribulation resistance. A fortune knot is an unpaid deposit that landed. Same substance, different size, different bill. The trait does not upgrade into a knot, and standing near a knot does not switch the trait off.

Mortals through Dao Manifestation do not have this explanation. They tell the novel. v1 chronicle should speak in **rumor voice** ("heaven favors that junior") and leave the snag as something a hermit, a record, or a much later tribulation can say more honestly. The player is allowed to believe the rumor for a long time.

What this rejects:

- Heaven writing a manuscript, picking a protagonist, or preferring the righteous.
- A rubber band that appears because the player is succeeding.
- Plot armor that refills.

An Immortal holding fortune out of the cycle forever is still the dam problem in the cosmology doc. A mortal knot is a pebble jam. It is allowed because it ends.

## Why this is a different animal from a Demonic Talent

Demonic Talents already ship (`NPC_ROLES.demonic_talent`, occupation string "Heaven-defying prodigy"). They are geniuses who **break procedure**: fast growth, a threat meter, a confront-or-bargain quest, a Dao alignment hit when you kill or join them. Their talk is "the heavens wrote rules. I chose to edit them."

A fortune knot does not edit the rules and is not edited *for*. No demonic qi, no bargain, no corruption. Killing one is cutting a rock out of a stream. The social price is fame, a patron's grudge, and — if the lump was still thick — one sharp splash when the deposit returns at once. Dao alignment does not move.

| | Demonic Talent (shipped) | Fortune knot (this doc) |
|--|--------------------------|-------------------------|
| Relation to the rules | They defy procedure | A lump of fortune has snagged on them |
| Feel | Villain prodigy, unnatural growth | The person chances keep finishing |
| Tell | Demonic qi, threat, 😈 | Ordinary face; the room leans their way |
| Power | Higher growth rate, slightly thicker combat | Events: escapes, chances, pressure breakthroughs |
| Player menu | Confront or bargain | Race the chance, refuse the duel, strike when the deposit is thin, or stand beside them |
| Death | A real kill, alignment shifts | A real kill **after the deposit is spent**. No alignment hit |
| How common | Rare wanderer, ~10 year emergence | Scarcer. One thickness per band. Not a role weight |

A talented disciple, a rival who scales to you, and a sect ecology `prodigy` are a third category: **skill and ceiling**. They can be brilliant and still have an ordinary trickle of fortune. Do not fold them into this flag. A knot may join a sect and occupy its prodigy slot. The slot can also be a hard-working genius with no deposit at all.

Leave the demonic occupation string alone until a copy pass. "Heaven-defying" on that role means defiance. Do not reuse those talk lines here.

Folk will still say Blessed. The doc keeps that word for the rumor. The flag, if it is ever built, should be named for the knot, not for favor.

## What the two novels are doing

The novels supply the **shape**. They do not supply the cause. Both of those stories run on a heaven that can play favorites. This one cannot. Steal the scenes, replace the patron.

**History's Strongest Senior Brother.** Opportunities walk into someone, and a straight fight while the deposit is thick is how bystanders get written out of the hour — because the lump spends itself on survival and comprehension. The senior brother's answer still works, retold as temperament: do not stand where the drip is about to land, do not duel on the ground where the deposit can complete a chapter, and do not assume "stronger today" means "the blow will land." They die when the deposit is thin, starved of new snags, or simply overmatched.

**Paragon of Sin.** Fortuitous encounters, a body a peer cannot finish, a breakthrough snatched out of a defeat. That is the prodigy daydream. The sting is meeting someone who is living a thicker deposit than your trait.

The player who wants to *be* that life feels the mountain. The player who wants to *survive* it gets the patient game: take the chance, wait, then one blow.

## The adaptation rule

**A fortune knot is a finite deposit. It buys scenes while it lasts. It never buys a second life after a confirmed death, and nothing refills it for free.**

| Brake | Means |
|-------|--------|
| **Budget** | A small integer, `fortune`. Escapes and chances spend it. It does not regen inside a fight. It does not regen for staying alive. A later snag is a new event. |
| **Peer scope** | The absurd survival is for someone in your range. A two-realm gap is more than the drip can pay. |
| **Death sticks** | At zero, or after the escapes this fight were allowed, they die like any other NPC. No hidden extra life. No return from the dead. Lifespan is the normal one for their realm. Hard to kill is not long-lived. |

If a proposal needs a chooser, infinite plot armor, a player debuff, or "they always have one more chance," it has left this doc.

## Fortune

Hidden integer on the NPC. The player does not see the number at first. They see the scenes, then later a sense of thick / thin / spent.

| Band | Deposit | Escapes in one fight | Who they are |
|------|---------|----------------------|--------------|
| **Local knot** | 3 | 1 | County genius, lesser-sect junior. The first one a Dustbone prodigy should meet. A decade of uncanny luck, then ordinary, unless a new snag lands. |
| **Regional knot** | 8 | 2 | The name the zone chronicle will not shut up about. A great-sect disciple or a wanderer whose deposit has started to stack events. |
| **Age knot** | 16+ | not for v1 | One large jam in an age. Rumor only until upper realms. You do not roll this as a road encounter. |

**Spends**

| Scene | Cost | What the player sees |
|-------|------|----------------------|
| Narrow escape | −1 | The blow that should have ended them does not. The fight **ends** with them alive and gone — cliff and spirit herb, a senior's sleeve, the beast turning, a hidden jade cracking instead of the heart. They do not get a free turn that kills you. |
| Chance claimed | −1 | Inheritance, patron, beast core, sealed ground. They receive one concrete boon (growth, a patron flag, a named treasure). The deposit dripped out as an event. |
| Comprehension under pressure | −1 | Cornered or fresh from an escape, they break through, or their growth jumps a visible step. |
| Comeback after a public humiliation | −2 | The remaining lump is under pressure and spends itself on a recovery event, later. If it cannot pay, nothing happens. There is no revenge written for them. They are simply easier to kill. |

**No topping up**

Staying alive does not restore fortune. Winning against the player does not restore it. The higher mountain is a **larger knot already in the world**, not a new shield bolted on after you look clever.

Do not refund an escape five minutes later. That point has re-entered the cycle. That is the window.

## How a killing blow resolves

On a hit that would drop them to 0:

1. **Fortune remains, and this fight still has an escape.** Spend 1. Set them to a sliver or remove them from the field. Log the absurd survival. Combat ends. They are injured in the fiction, alive in the save, and poorer.
2. **Fortune is 0, or this fight already used its escapes.** They die. The log is ordinary. No shudder. No second roll.
3. **Player realm is two or more above theirs.** Deny the escape. A Foundation cultivator executing a Qi Condensation junior is allowed to succeed.
4. **The deposit was already spent this season** (a recent escape, a chance just claimed, a comeback already paid). This encounter has nothing left. Death sticks at peer realm too, if you land the blow.

Prepared ground can stand in for the gap: no audience, a sealed place, they are already spent. v1 can skip a special execution action and just honor (3) and (4).

Confirmed death is the end of the flag. Do not spawn a new knot from the corpse. The deposit returns to the cycle. It does not automatically snag on the killer or on a spare child. `spawnVengeanceDemonicTalent` is the wrong follow-up. A grieving friend or a patron's hunter can come later, through situation threads. That person is angry. They are not the new knot unless a separate snag says so.

## Chances, and the race

Do not build the universal "anyone can ascend" pulse parked on the Redwell docs. One flagged person is enough.

While a knot lives and still has fortune, the world scheduler occasionally offers a spend (local: every few years; regional: a little faster):

- A place (cave, ruin, lot) the player can reach.
- A patron or a closed-door rumor the player cannot physically steal, only arrive too late to matter.

If it is a place, the player hears a stir **before** the chronicle awards it. Take it — explore, buy, claim — and their chance fizzles. Fortune does not drop (the drip never completed). They, or someone who likes them, may notice.

Ignore it, and the chronicle records that they found it. Fortune −1. They get the boon. The rumor will say heaven handed it to them.

That is the prodigy pressure in one loop. Your run assumed the next rare thing was yours. A deposit nearby is the default recipient unless you move.

## How the player comes to know

No quest titled after them.

| Phase | What you have |
|-------|----------------|
| Rumor | "A junior survived the scorpion pit. Third time this year." No name required. The line may say heaven favors them. That line is gossip. |
| Name | Gossip acquires a person. Easy to dismiss as a braggart. |
| Meeting | You see them. Qi is not demonic. Merchants offer them the better price. The stray beast does not bare teeth first. Their role label is still disciple, wanderer, rival — whatever they actually are. |
| Witness | You watch an escape, or you lose a chance you were walking toward. |
| Sense (later, higher realm) | Thick, thin, or spent. A high enough eye might see a snag rather than a blessing. v1 can live without this. |

"Blessed" is the whisper. The occupation stays ordinary until the pattern is obvious. A drainage ditch often looks like a lucky junior.

## The prodigy player's mountain

Heavenly Luck stays a personal bonus. Comparison is the hurt, not a silent debuff.

When a knot shares your zone and you have been winning — early breakthroughs, fame, the trait, a tournament later:

- Talk from elders cools. "We thought you were the one." They mean the rumor.
- Contested chances default to the knot if you delay. Ordinary herb gathering does not.
- A peer duel while the deposit is thick becomes their comprehension. You were standing where a drip needed to land.

| Life | What you are doing |
|------|--------------------|
| **The prodigy** | Race every chance. Refuse the duel the deposit can complete. Kill only when it is thin, and accept the fame and the grudge. |
| **The steady senior** | Do not stand in the drip. Let them have a loud chance you do not need. Strike once, on prepared ground, or never. |
| **The person beside the knot** | Stand close. A splash sometimes lands on you. Rare, telegraphed beats may ask someone adjacent to pay a price (time, blood, a resource). Stepping out is always available. The unwind does not get to kill the player as a cutscene. |

Ignoring them is legal. They spend the deposit and climb, or they spend it and become ordinary. The chronicle mentions the loud version. When a thicker knot breaks a realm you have not, the higher heaven stops being a proverb.

**The higher heaven is a larger deposit already elsewhere, not a respawn.** Cut the local knot and the regional one is already a rumor from Jade or the Heartlands. The age knot stays a name in an old text. v1 ships the local knot as a person and the next band as rumor.

## Death, residue, and company

**Killing a knot**

- Fame, sized to the band. A local knot is a louder rival kill, not a story-boss.
- No Dao alignment shift.
- If they had a sect or a patron, a grudge can open later (situation threads). v1 can be one log line plus, at most, an ordinary vengeful NPC.
- Residue: if the deposit was already spent, the return is quiet. Maybe a single contested leftover at the site (a cracked jade, a half-claimed insight). If you cut a **thick** deposit, the lump hits the cycle all at once: the chronicle is loud for a season, and the next tribulation is slightly sharper, once. That is standing in the flood. It is not heaven resenting a favorite's killer, not a curse, not a new trait, and not a transfer of the knot onto you.

**Sparing or standing close** does not move their flag onto you. Splash boons stay small and occasional.

**Old age and ordinary failure** still kill them. Fortune is not lifespan. A local knot who spends out, or who meets a calamity larger than the deposit, can die without you. The world is not obligated to keep them until you arrive.

**A knot who also walks into demonic cultivation** is a parked calamity. v1: the two flags never share a person.

## What this should not become

- A second Demonic Talent with a higher HP multiplier. The feel is the escape and the stolen chance.
- A role weight in `ZONE_NPC_ROLES`. They are not 5% of wanderers.
- A regen timer, or a rubber band on the player's success.
- A main-quest arrow.
- A player transformation. The trait, the manuals, and the player's own choices are already the prodigy life. Wearing a knot would erase the mountain.
- The parked universal opportunity sim. One scheduler thread, one NPC.
- Permission for an unwind to kill the player as a cutscene. Player death stays combat.
- A heaven that prefers them. If a line needs a chooser to make sense, cut the line.

## First slice, when someone builds it

1. After the player has had time to feel clever (not day one), one **local knot** in their region. Cap: one per zone. The regional knot is rumor only.
2. Fortune 3. One escape per fight. The escape ends the fight. No regen.
3. Two chance types: a place the player can race, and a breakthrough rumor.
4. Chronicle lines in rumor voice, plus one quieter line on death if you want the snag to be guessable ("the luck went out of them like a held breath").
5. At fortune 0, combat kills them for good. A two-realm gap denies the escape.
6. Fame on the kill. No alignment shift. No new knot from the body.
7. Heavenly Luck untouched.

Regional knot as a real NPC, a sense that can tell snag from blessing, patron grudges, splash fortune, and the age knot gaining a body all wait. World Fortune stays the immortal-scale meter. Do not wire this integer into it on day one. They should stay compatible: a pebble jam and a dam, same water.

## Open questions

- **Is the snag the right cause?** The three rows are one suggestion. A single cause (only vacuum drains, for example) would be easier to explain and less like a list of excuses.
- **When may the player hear the true cause?** Proposal: rumor for a long time; a hermit or a record can say "snag" without a lecture. Full cosmology stays late.
- **Does public humiliation do anything by itself?** Proposal: it only triggers a comeback if fortune can pay. Otherwise the person is merely embarrassed and thinner. Leave special "crack their destiny" actions out of v1.
- **Can NPCs mistake Heavenly Luck for a knot?** Flavor only ("the well has two lucky ones this generation"). Mechanically the player remains a person with a trait.
- **Gender, age, path.** Not all young masters. v1 can roll a normal world NPC and set the flag.
- **Intersection with rivals.** The rival role is their opinion of you. The knot is a deposit. Both can be true. Do not require it.
- **Named or rolled.** Lean rolled, with chronicle voice doing the literature. Redwell / Dustbone is the right *band* for the first one.
