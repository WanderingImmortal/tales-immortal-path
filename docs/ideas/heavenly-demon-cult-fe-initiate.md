Parent ladder: [`heavenly-demon-cult-life.md`](heavenly-demon-cult-life.md). Awakening: [`devouring-intent.md`](devouring-intent.md). ## Layer 2 — Stained Foundation (染基篇)

Second layer of **Heavenly Demon Canon** (`heavenly_demon_devour_line`). Swaps in at Initiate rank; shares layer progress with later tiers per [`cultivation-manuals-framework.md`](cultivation-manuals-framework.md).

### What the manual teaches

| Slot | Content (working) | Role |
|------|-------------------|------|
| **Circulation** | **Stained Circulation** (ch.1) — upgrade from Outer Devouring Breath | FE gather/refine/stabilize fantasy; apex cult quality, **no stability malus** |

### Stained Circulation — effects (design)

| Effect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Gather** | Strong FE method — competes with orthodox heir breaths |
| **Stability** | **No manual penalty** — same lock as Layer 1 |

**Not in Layer 2:** Devouring Intent techniques (Feeder / Layer 3), law fragments, meridian canon.

## Fasting meditations

Not a punishment minigame — **flavor months** that pace FE and teach doctrine.

| Beat | Cadence | Feel |
|------|---------|------|
| **Minor fast** | Every **6–12 months** (calendar) | Skip one "comfort" action month — no inn luxury, no feast event; +small cultivate focus or groundwork tick |
| **Hall fast** (main only) | Elder assigns **1× per year** | Group meditation in initiate hall; absence = −merit |
| **Gorge fast** | Before seal attempt | 1–3 month chain — *"Empty the dantian of borrowed qi."* |
| **Break fast wrong** | Eat feast / pill binge mid-fast | No hard fail — elder line: *"You fed the wrong mouth."* Small merit loss |

**Groundwork hook:** completing fasts counts toward intent groundwork (one of the "walked the road" checks in [`devouring-intent.md`](devouring-intent.md)).

## Life loops at FE

Pacing: inferior root may spend **decades** as Initiate (~30–50 years in FE band before peak ~80–90). Loops must **carry** long calendars — not QC-speed.

### Monthly loop (main gorge)

```
1. Cultivate   — initiate hall slot or assigned chamber time (Layer 2)
2. Drill       — spar under elder eye (merit, groundwork)
3. Assignment  — hunt team / escort / salvage (rotation)
4. Ledger      — taking log updated (sanctioned kills, tasks)
5. Tithe accrual — gorge quota ticks toward 3-month due date
6. Rest        — initiate hall (basic residence — better than outer)
```

### Monthly loop (branch)

```
1. Cultivate   — alone; safehouse or rented room if exposed
2. Cell task   — delivery, collection, field work (higher stakes than QC)
3. Hunt        — often solo or with one "brother" — log kills for merit
4. Tithe accrual — same 3-month window; FE amount hurts
5. Rent / job  — Redwell economy still bites ([`redwell-starter-city.md`](redwell-starter-city.md))
6. Rumor check — bounty tier 1; healer prices; orthodox side-eye
```

### Quarterly loop (both — same tick as QC outer)

| Beat | Initiate change |
|------|-----------------|
| **Tithe due** | **Higher** stones/reagents than outer; miss twice → disown (worse: hunted) |
| **Blood pill pool** | Still **1 per 4** — but pool is **initiates + outers** on main; branch pool is whole cell |
| **Merit ledger** | Hunt yield, drill wins, fast completion, tithe on time |
| **Envoy / elder review** | Branch: transfer pitch; Main: Feeder candidacy notes |

### Yearly beats

| Beat | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Fasting week** | Main: mandatory once; Branch: optional but groundwork-weak without |
| **Sanctioned hunt season** | Cell sends teams — **best merit season** for initiates |
| **Bounty season** (branch) | Random orthodox patrol event — hide arts, forged papers |
| **Gorge pilgrimage** (branch) | Optional until seal / Layer 2 complete pages require it |

## Promotion Initiate → Feeder

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
