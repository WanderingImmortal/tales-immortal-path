# Innate gifts and creation points

Updated: 2026-11-28. Source: author design discussion of the same date. Design only; not an implementation commitment.

## Accepted direction

Creation points should support both conventional improvements (such as better spiritual roots) and exotic components of a build, including meaningful burdens and unusual life circumstances. Gifts create opportunities rather than prescribe classes. Some may grant genuinely exclusive actions, perceptions or mechanics; ordinary cultivation need not reproduce every gift. Others may be approximated through difficult learned methods. Apply the same underlying rules to NPCs and players. Costs, unlock conditions, drawback refunds and the metaphysical explanation of reincarnation remain open.

## Twin Aspect Soul

Accepted core: two naturally complete souls belonging to the same person, rather than one reserve divided in two. Both mature alongside one another from birth and share identity and life experience. Both can independently think, acquire knowledge, form memories and share them. One is primary for direct player control; no requirement to support two directly controlled characters in different places.

Both may cultivate simultaneously and independently, with separate progress and resource requirements. Two souls do not grant two bodies or automatic advanced soul abilities. Destruction of an aspect is permanent; regeneration is not part of this gift. A separate renewal gift could eventually interact with it, but is not designed.

The gift may go unnoticed for an entire ordinary life. Conscious exploitation depends on acquired soul capabilities, not merely possession of two souls. Exact early manifestations, discovery gates, action compatibility, memory synchronisation, survival after primary destruction and independent manifestation remain open. Reconcile innate soul terminology with the spirit path's Soul Birth milestone before implementation; this does not change existing realm gates.

Artificial analogue: [Soul shearing](soul-shearing.md), deliberately much less desirable. Historical extreme: [Soul King](war-legends.md).

## Other candidate gifts (exploratory)

- Thunder Tempering Body: convert some absorbed lightning into bodily refinement; capacity and injury limits, no inherent attraction or generation. Distinct from Lightning Attractor and lightning Qi affinity.
- Yin-Yang Celestial Eyes: perceive Yin and Yang aspects of phenomena; perception does not confer comprehension. Exact applications remain open.
- Renewal soul: possible separate component, not a regeneration rule for Twin Aspect Soul.
