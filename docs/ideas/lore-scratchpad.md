## Sword Immortal — the Solitary Sword Path diary

**Provisional idea:** The *Solitary Sword Path* is not necessarily a polished supreme jade-slip manual. It may be the Sword Immortal's own battered, handwritten diary: an ordinary old book with worn pages, corrections, failed experiments, and mundane observations, held together only by the lingering breath/power of an Immortal. He still writes in it with brush and ink.

The **Celestial Sword Canon** is the cultivated, teachable sect inheritance; the diary records what the Sword Immortal is *still* struggling to understand beyond it. The solitude refers to his pursuit of sword cultivation without predecessors to guide him, not necessarily social isolation. He might share insights without the diary itself becoming a miraculous transferable cultivation method.

### Da Chi's interruptions

The Mad Arhat Da Chi repeatedly arrives seeking a fight, disrupting the Sword Immortal's attempts at contemplation. Da Chi may regard them as close friends; the Sword Immortal records his exasperation. An occasional interruption might accidentally prompt an insight, which the Sword Immortal is reluctant to credit.
