## Intent

**无迹大道** *Wú Jì Dào* — **Traceless Great Dao** — cultivation path whose apex is **unholdable presence**: no dossier tail, no clean tracking, no **trace** for heaven’s ledger to grab. **Standalone Great Dao** (layer 2), **yin-adjacent** on the axis but **not** “part of Yin Maiden doctrine” and **not** gendered.

**Distinct from:** [`disguise-and-public-identity.md`](disguise-and-public-identity.md) (player cover + signature ledger) — Traceless is **embodied world rule**, not makeup.

## Rule sentence

*What is not traced cannot be held.*
