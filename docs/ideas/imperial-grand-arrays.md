## Nine Dragons Seal the Capital (九龙镇京阵)

**Working English:** **Nine Dragons Array** · **Nine Dragons Guard the Ancestral Grounds**  
**Hanzi:** 九龙镇京阵 *(array)* · 九龙守京 *(motif / cult phrase)*

**Not the same as:** the **five-clawed true dragon** on imperial regalia — five claws = **Tian mandate** on living authority. **Nine dragons** = **pre-mandate ancestral array grammar** — guardians bound to **veins and palace geometry**, not a count of Immortals.

### What it is

A **grand array** predating the Dao Wars settlement — older than Longcheng, older than the charter. The Tian clan did not invent dragon qi on the continent; they **inherited a ruin**, re-threaded it through **underpalace veins**, and made it the **spine of Tianjing**.

Nine **dragon nodes** sit on the ancestral grounds:

| # | Anchor (fiction) | Role in the array |
|---|------------------|-------------------|
| 1–4 | **Palace mounts** at the inner wall (cardinal gates) | **Coil** — turn assault lines; Avatars that press the wall meet **counter-weight** |
| 5–8 | **Vein junctions** under ministry rings | **Drink** — bleed grand techniques and formation fuel; siege arrays **starve** faster than attackers expect |
| 9 | **Ancestral cauldron / dragon throne** (palace mount, not public) | **Heart** — holds the pattern; today **nested** with the sleeping founder's **seal anchor** (underpalace stub) |

Visually: when the array runs, the jianghu sees **nine qi-dragons** (five claws on **none** of them in public art — **four claws** in stone, five claws reserved for Tian **persons**) rise from the ring and **interlock** over the ancestral grounds. Inner court poetry: *"Nine watch; one sleeps."*

### What it did at Tianjing (Dao Wars — owner lock)

The coalition that marched on Tianjing fielded **peak Dao Manifestation** assets (Sword Ancestor, Flame Emperor, demon harvest columns, lesser-clan hosts). Their **Celestial Avatars** and **grand battle formations** could flatten ordinary capitals.

**The Nine Dragons did not make Tianjing invincible forever.** It did three things chroniclers still argue about:

1. **Partitioned** — assault force could not **concentrate** on the underpalace breakthrough cordon; nine nodes forced **multiple breaches**, each expensive.
2. **Contested Avatars** — raised Avatars on the siege line did not shatter; they **guttered** — clarity collapsed, techniques dampened, as if the sky over Tianjing **refused** a second sun ([`dao-wars-capital-turn.md`](dao-wars-capital-turn.md) Avatar hook; [`celestial-avatar.md`](celestial-avatar.md) clash vs **pre-laid array**).
3. **Bought one beat too long** — enough minutes (or hours — deliberately vague) for the genius inside to **complete Half-Step** before the inner gate fell.

After the **garden walk**, the array was **not** what broke the coalition — **Half-Step** did. Phoenix and Sword records agree: *the dragons held; then the board changed.*

### What it is now

- **Dormant** under normal peace — maintained by an **Array Ministry** (inner Tianjing) and a **Formations Guild contract** for node repairs (guild stamps **components**; Tian **owns** the pattern).
- **Partial wake** on existential threat — inner gate seal, Dragon Guard muster, Pei Yin's key; **full nine** last seen at Tianjing in war chronicles.
- **Founder sleep** — post-mandate ascension tied the **Immortal's restraint** into the **ninth node** so his **held qi** does not drain the Heartlands like an active Immortal walking the map ([`heaven-cycle-and-apexes.md`](heaven-cycle-and-apexes.md)). Waking him **is** waking the heart dragon.

### Comparison — Sword mountain

| | **Nine Dragons (Tianjing)** | **Ten Thousand Swords Seal (Solitary Sword Mountain)** |
|--|------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------|
| **Metaphor** | Guardians · coils · veins | Blades · sepulcher · one line |
| **War job** | **Delay** coalition; gutter Avatars | **Deny** entry after recede; kill geometry if forced |
| **Apex tie** | Sleeping **Tian founder** (Immortal, restrained) | Sleeping **Sword Immortal** |
| **Half-Step** | Held the **siege** | **Pays** to force the peak (charter deterrence today) |

Mutual deterrence: neither side advertises a casual test.

## Fiction guardrails (don't break the board)

- **Does not** replace Half-Step as the war-ending ceiling — it **enabled** the breakthrough moment.
- **Does not** auto-win against **Half-Step** or **Immortal** — today, waking the founder is the tier that moves continents.
- **Does not** teach nine dragon techniques to every Tian soldier — it's **infrastructure**, not a martial manual ([`imperial-clan.md`](imperial-clan.md) cultivation still TBD).
- **Dragon's Edict** (龙敕) at Ember Crown is **founder art**, **not** a node of this array ([`dao-wars-capital-turn.md`](dao-wars-capital-turn.md)).
