## Intent

With a **working body path**, the cult is still **qi-primary** (Canon, intent, law) — but gains a **body sector**: military-industrial flesh production, not a third equal pillar. **Soul** stays pit-whisper only (no public soul academy).

Park **death squads**, blood-pill rapid tempering, loyalty seals, and elite **blood-regen Reavers** — cult-coded, **not** Vajra Ridge copy.

---

## Cult three-lane model (idealized)

```text
QI (spine)     — Heavenly Demon Canon, Devouring Intent, Devouring Law
BODY (sector)  — Blood Vessel Ward — fodder, reavers, regen predators
SOUL (whisper) — forbidden / pit research only — NOT taught openly
```

| Lane | Public? | Owner |
|------|---------|-------|
| Qi | Yes — all sworn disciples | Canon + merit |
| Body | Yes — ward recruits | Cauldron + drill instructors |
| Soul | **No** — sacrilege lane | Founder pit only |

## Three ward tiers

### 1 — Expendables ("meat heads")

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Source** | Bad roots, captives, failed outer recruits |
| **Method** | Blood pill stacks + crude tempering → **Bronze Skin** fast |
| **Loyalty** | **Blood bind seal** — break = meridian rupture (not affection) |
| **Role** | Diversion squads, siege ladders, walk-at-Sword-patrol fodder |
| **Doctrine** | *"The maw does not throw away meat it already swallowed"* applies to **Reavers**, **not** these |

### 2 — Blood Reavers (body-armed predators)

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Realm** | **GC+** typical |
| **Lane** | Canon **+** vessel tempering |
| **Combat** | **Regen mid-fight**; wounds clot wrong; hard to finish |
| **Status** | Merit-ranked **inventory** — investment, not sermon gods |
| **Spar culture** | Yield default; death match **sanctioned** ([`heavenly-demon-cult-gc-reaver.md`](heavenly-demon-cult-gc-reaver.md)) |

### 3 — Pit-adjacent (founder only)

Failures and **anomalies** the ward cannot process — including **Pit Two** ([`heavenly-demon-cult-inner-court.md`](heavenly-demon-cult-inner-court.md)). Mo Xuan may not control this floor fully.
