## Intent

Flagship **orthodox** great sect of the **Jade Tide Archipelago** — storm / lightning / dragon-sovereignty dao. A real cultivation sect that happens to sit on islands and benefits from tide-road trade — **not a navy**, not a shipping company.

Replaces thin `storm_dragon` stub in `data.js` when owner promotes.

## Design sentence

> Storm-path cultivators whose grand array stabilizes the archipelago’s **tide-roads**; lightning and dragon are how they hold the chain — not their job title.

## Geography

- **Throne island** — main hall, keystone spire (hub players name)
- **Array nodes** — smaller isles; formation / meditation sites, not naval bases
- **Tide-roads** — spirit currents between islands; sect stabilizes via keystone + storm doctrine
- Dao is **not** “only works in archipelago” — sect and keystone are **here**; cultivator not zone-locked

## Load-bearing pin

**They keep the tide-roads open** — cultivation + keystone array, not maritime industry. Trade and safe passage are **consequences**.

## Leadership (shell)

| Layer | Sketch |

| **Public** | Patriarch / Route Marshal — VR peak; runs hall, charter talk |
| **Apex** | **The Storm Dragon** — immortal epithet; distinct **character**, not interchangeable vault sleeper |

### Storm Dragon character (owner lean — tweaks welcome)

- **Temper:** Quick to anger, not unreasonable — lightning flares and passes
- **Pride:** Storm dao and what he holds; impatient with small thinking — not “mortals treat us like shippers”
- **Immortal life:** Mostly on storm peak — **cultivating through the array** (holding the pin *is* his path)
- **Moves rarely:** Intervenes when nodes fail or immortal-tier threat; each act costly (heaven’s debt / restrictions)
- **Not:** regional rain god; not under the sea (Lotus lane); not eternal sailor
