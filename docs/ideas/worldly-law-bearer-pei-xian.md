## Personality

- **Survivor:** family/clan **gone generations** — war, road, no bluff left to maintain (contrast [`worldly-law-bearer-gu-dusheng.md`](worldly-law-bearer-gu-dusheng.md)).
- **Solitude:** endures **indignity**; **won’t** die on pride; **will** die on boredom.
- **Petty thief:** steals **small** — stones pouch tail, spice, dice, a **flashy but useless** trinket; **fun**, not rent.
- **Robbery code (owner lock):** **never** last heal, quest macguffin, or sole manual page; often leaves a **clue** (riddle, dropped chit, “ask the tofu woman”) so a **sharp or kind** mark can **recover** it — **game for him**, not cruelty.
- **Help:** still aids **nobodies** sometimes — not Gu’s warmth; **mischief + spare coin** energy.

## Law — **Traceless Great Dao** (无迹大道 *Wú Jì Dào*)

### Taxonomy (not Disguise, not “female yin only”)

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **Own Great Dao?** | **Yes** — **无迹大道** *Traceless Great Dao* (standalone path). |
| **Under Yin?** | **Yin-adjacent** on the **axis** (stillness, reception, hiding) — **not** a subset of **Yin Maiden** curriculum. **Yin Dao** 太阴大道 is a **different** Great Dao ([`frostbite-yin-sect.md`](frostbite-yin-sect.md) sect walks **yin peak** — **not** gender-locked cosmology; that sect is **all-female by institution**, not because **yin = women**). |
| **Under Shadow?** | Optional future **影道** *Shadow Dao* as **sibling** or **lesser way** — Pei **does not** need it for v1 lore. |
| **Others can walk it?** | **Yes** — assassin halls, thief guilds, a few yin-adjacent hermits; **Pei** is **memorable**, not unique in history. |
| **OP?** | Great Dao at **mid–peak depth**, not Half-Step; **fill**-limited; **countered** by illuminate, true-name reads, array-locked rooms ([`dao-combat-target-spine.md`](dao-combat-target-spine.md)). |

**Rule sentence:** *What is not traced cannot be held.*

**Player-visible wear:**

| Situation | Effect (lean) |
|-----------|----------------|
| Passive | **Sense / dossier** reads **mortal beggar** until he **commits fill** |
| Combat | **Target lock**, **tracking**, **fate tail** chips **decay** in his imposed patch |
| Flight | **Withdraw** from engagement band — contest via geometry, not RPS |
| **Not** | MC **disguise UI** — different system ([`disguise-and-public-identity.md`](disguise-and-public-identity.md)) |

**Lesser ways (others / his past):** **垢衣诀** *Gòuyī Jué* Mortal Skin, **窄门诀** *Zhǎi Mén Jué* Alley Exit — Pei **completed the Greater**, not “only a trick lesser.”
