## Intent

**残编大道** *Cán Biān Dà Dào* — **Remnant Plot Great Dao** — path whose Manifestation **forces unfinished stories toward an ending**: broken oaths, half-written vendettas, **aborted tribulations**, grudges that **should** have died in the Dao War but **didn’t**.

**Feel:** wrong, theatrical, **era scar** — not random chaos; **narrative gravity**.

**Distinct from:** Fate/karma systems ([`fate-fortune-grounding.md`](fate-fortune-grounding.md)) — **local**, **embodied**, **dramatic**, not ledger accounting.

## Rule sentence

*What was left unfinished must **play out**.*
