## Intent

Park **who runs the cult above branch cells** — not the player join loop. Three layers of succession fiction (myth / admin / pit), **seven public disciples** competing for **Patriarch** (Mo Xuan's chair), and **two pit specimens** the founder keeps off the merit board.

**Not heirs to the Heavenly Demon.** The stage mask is a **blood clone**; the pit holds the **founder**. Admin succession is real; god succession is theater.

## Three locks (succession model)

| Lock | Who believes | Prize | Truth |
|------|--------------|-------|-------|
| **Outer myth** | Juniors, propaganda | "Next Heavenly Demon rises from the pit" | **Ruse** — motivates grunts ([`blood-sealing-gorge.md`](blood-sealing-gorge.md)) |
| **Middle admin** | Inner court, seven disciples | **Patriarch** — Mo Xuan's seals, branches, pulpit schedule | **Real** human job — stable, valuable, unglamorous |
| **Inner research** | Pit attendants, founder, Mo Xuan (partial) | **Specimens** — rule-breakers in a box | **Not** succession — founder R&D |

```text
MYTH  →  Heavenly Demon (clone / sermon face)
ADMIN →  Mo Xuan + seven (state arm)
TRUTH →  Founder in pit + two specimens
```

**Author rule:** Nobody **becomes** the Heavenly Demon through discipleship. Clone is **manufactured**; specimens are **contained**.

**Pit pair vs Patriarch (owner lock):** Gu Wei and Tuo Yan are **not** competing for Mo Xuan's chair — **not** in the seven, **not** on the merit board, **not** succession candidates. The seven fight admin; the pit holds founder R&D. Gu Wei would **use** the game if she met it; Tuo Yan would treat Patriarch as **paperwork and thinking** — a hassle he'd ignore unless the founder **obligated** him.

### Succession & banner war (owner lock 2026-08-30)

**No formal rank ladder** (#1–#7 power order). Mo Xuan parcels **revocable seals** and **access**; disciples hoard **banners** in the shadows.

```text
SPINE (not for sale)     Mo Xuan · Hong Lian · withdrawal / array core
COURT (late commits)     Major post-holders — visible; need major reason to flip
CHAFF (banner fodder)    Retired plateau · menial posts · hermits · failed climbers
LEGIONS (disciple-owned) Personal armies — stockpiled in dormancy; archetype-flavored
```

| Phase | What happens |
|-------|----------------|
| **Mo Xuan alive** | Collect chaff elders + train legions; court major elders quietly; **hide cards** |
| **Mo Xuan dies** | Cascade — chaff bandwagons, major elders commit, seal scramble |
| **Founder** | Indifferent to infighting unless pit feed / silence threatened — **strong rise, weak culled** |

Mo Xuan **allows** banner-building as training; **punishes** threats to withdrawal or gorge spine. Assassination is deniable, rare, and costly.

**Chaff elders** — small prestige each; menial access (ration logs, rotations, safe houses); cheap to court; disciples wary of showing hoard size. **Major elder** defection = everyone notices.
