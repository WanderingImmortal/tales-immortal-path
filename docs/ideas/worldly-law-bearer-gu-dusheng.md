## Personality

- **Now:** gentle, selective help, **won’t** rebuild clan or play charter politics; “beyond factions” = **won’t adjudicate the board**, still **good-leaning**.
- **Then:** smiled on the field — decay as **settling the field**, not rage.
- **Secret (simple):** he **left**; top kin **hide** it; he **won’t out them** — last kindness.
- **Knows** cousins still forge letters; **doesn’t** debunk — bluff is load-bearing for kin.
