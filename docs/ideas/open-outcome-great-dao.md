## Intent

**未结局大道** *Wèi Jié Jú Dà Dào* — **Open Outcome Great Dao** — Manifestation that **refuses closure**: fights, debts, oaths, and “finished” business **stay live** in the holder’s volume.

**Feel:** **powerhouse wrongness** — cheerful protocol, **investigate-able** props; inverse of **Decay** (Gu settles the field).

**Supersedes for Fu:** [`unstand-down-great-dao.md`](unstand-down-great-dao.md) (army clerk — too small).

## Domain legislation (plain)

Inside embodied volume (F3 target):

| Effect | Player read |
|--------|-------------|
| **No clean finish** | Kill / surrender / flee resolution **delayed one beat** or needs **contest win** vs his depth+fill |
| **Wins don’t land** | Post-fight boons, “quest complete” satisfaction, morale — **thin** until you leave volume or force close |
| **Losses nag** | Grudge / rematch **situation-thread** weight; old wounds **re-ache** (light) |
| **Dead not filed** | Corpses read as **still in play** to *him* — bow, stake, toast; not necromancy by default |
| **Mis-seating** | Wrong **rank/name** on lists — social danger, investigate watermark |
