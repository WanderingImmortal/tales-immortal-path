## Intent

**军律大道** *Jūn Lǜ Dà Dào* — **Field Law Great Dao** — Manifestation that **only recognizes battlefield law**: challenge, quarter, parley, requisition, treatment of dead — **not** town custom, charter, or “we’re at peace now.”

**Wild (狂), not mad (疯):** **extreme**, **unreasonable in peacetime**, **one codex applied everywhere** — still **legible** and **consistent**.

**Player fairness:** does **not** steal earned wins. It **judges conduct** and **punishes violations**; obey the code → respect / passage / clue.

**Not tied to Fu’s habits:** toast/teeth/bow are **life layer** ([`worldly-law-bearer-fu-yanxi.md`](worldly-law-bearer-fu-yanxi.md)) — brother jokes, grief ticks — **not** domain symptoms.

## Rule sentence

*Only the **field’s law** counts.*
