## Intent

**Founding a sect** = investment in something **greater than yourself** (world influence, disciples, politics). **Personal estate** = **your** anchor (rent/buy/hire) without requiring sect. **Neither** is the only meta progression.

## Paths (parallel)

| Path | Anchor | Influence |
|------|--------|-----------|
| **Nomad** | Travel kit only | Low local |
| **Hermit / estate** | `G.dwelling` — cave, house, manor | Personal |
| **Urban** | Shop, district (later) | Economic |
| **Sect** | Headquarters + grounds | Institutional / jianghu |

Sect residence bonus **layers on** personal dwelling — don't fold into one `G.sect.residence` only.
