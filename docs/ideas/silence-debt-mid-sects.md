## Intent

During the Dao Wars, Yin Maiden Palace **racked up silence debts** — concealment, severance strikes, yin expert deployments — from sects that **survived** capitulation. Some debts may **still be owed** today: awkward in charter peace, useful for **faction hooks** and **mid-tier sect** design.

**Not Lotus bonds** — no marriage web; **secret obligations** the debtor hopes everyone forgot.

## Debt flavors (templates)

| Debt type | Fiction | Game hook |
|-----------|---------|-----------|
| **Concealed retreat** | Maidens hid a losing army’s exit | Debtor sect elder still jumps when moon rises |
| **Severed champion** | Yin strike before a duel | Rival knows; blackmail risk |
| **Hidden breakthrough** | Tribulation concealed from audit | Heaven debt shared — Maidens hold proof |
| **Northern quell** | Maidens destroyed camped minor sect **for** client | Client owes “one silence” forever |
| **Failed pay** | Debtor couldn’t pay in war — **compound** obligation | Quest: fulfill or defy Maidens |
