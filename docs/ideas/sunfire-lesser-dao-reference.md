## Court of Noon (embodied domain)

**Inside volume:**

- Concealment tags fail (must be **in lit volume** for full riders — not global anti-stealth stat check).
- Regen / delayed heal weakened.
- New shields cost extra fill.
- **Tick:** Flesh drip + **Blind stack** (stack → miss / slow fill, not permanent skip).

**Holder:** Sunfire-shaped attacks pay normal fill; **holding domain** = baseline qi drain + spike when contested.

## Counterplay (required for fun)

Without these, domain is unbeatable — see target spine.

1. **Leave** — Withdraw to Far / out of volume (costs ATB + qi).
2. **Dampen** — mist, array, peer niche: slower blind stack, not immunity.
3. **Contest edge** — spend fill to shrink flare or suppress one tick.
4. **Punish holder** — holder often static / drained while expanded.
5. **Group** — kill holder; pull allies out; split in/outside party.

**Resist** = fight inside; **avoid** = not in volume — needs **movement**.
