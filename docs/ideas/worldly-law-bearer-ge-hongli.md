## Personality

- **Grumpy innkeeper without an inn** — **not** Gu’s gentleness, **not** Pei’s play.
- **Survivor:** clan **gone** in an earlier war generation — **chose** one vale and **never** left after Manifestation.
- **Hosts:** pays **one night**, **one pot of tea**, **no questions** if guest **keeps the fence law**.
- **Hates:** charter hunters who **demand** entry; bandits who **fire** first inside; **loud** disciples.

---

## Law — **Enclosure Great Dao** (囿道 *Yòu Dào*)

**Standalone Great Dao** (same tier as [`traceless-great-dao.md`](traceless-great-dao.md)) — **boundary as world rule**.

**Rule sentence:** *Inside my fence, **first blood** is forbidden; the boundary **remembers**.*

| Player sees | Meaning |
|-------------|---------|
| Enter vale (marked) | **Guest** chip if tea accepted |
| **First hostile** action inside | **Expel** to threshold + brief **Structure** push — not instant death |
| Fight **outside** | Ge may **not** pursue far — **Pin** anchor |
| Assassin vs guest | Guest who **draws first** **loses** chip; Ge **may** side with neither |

**Not RPS:** **Illuminate** / **Sever tether** / **array breach** contest per [`dao-combat-target-spine.md`](dao-combat-target-spine.md).

**Others on path:** charter **ward arrays**, other **囿** hermits — Ge is **one** memorable vale.

**Lesser ways (elsewhere):** **一门道** *One Gate* · **客绳道** *Guest Rope* — Ge took **full Enclosure**.
