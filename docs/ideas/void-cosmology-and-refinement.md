## Intent

**“Void” is not one thing.** Code and copy currently mix outer cosmos, passage geometry, cultivated qi, and pocket realms under one label. That blocks VR identity and the Peak VR → Dao Seeking breakthrough.

This doc **splits void into layers**, ties **Void Refinement** to the layers a mortal patriarch can actually work with, and parks **seeing past the void** as the Seeking gate.

Cross-links: [`void-temple-sect.md`](void-temple-sect.md), [`dao-seeking-and-manifestation.md`](dao-seeking-and-manifestation.md), [`realm-claims.md`](realm-claims.md) idx 5, [`post-immortal-cosmology.md`](post-immortal-cosmology.md).

## Void layers (owner lean)

```text
[ Law-layer ]              ← heaven’s rules in motion (not a place you walk)
       ↑ glimpse (Peak VR → Seeking)
[ Outer void ]             ← beyond firmament; pre-heaven; dao-fracture risk
       |
[ Firmament / sky shell ]  ← the “veil” — boundary of the mortal board
       |
[ Interstitial void ]      ← gaps between spaces (passage, blink)
       |
[ Mortal board ]           ← continent, seas, cities
       |
[ Void pockets ]           ← engineered void-space (prison, seals, arrays)
```

### 1 — Mortal board

Normal space. DT presence, NS sky travel, GC domain — all **inside** the board.

### 2 — Interstitial void (隙虚 · passage void)

The **gap between** locations — not outer cosmos. What **blink**, **void step**, and **gate transit** use.

- **VR grind (mid):** void arts learn to **ride** interstitial void for passage.
- **Feel:** geometry, shortcuts, skip encounters — realm claim **Passage**.
- **Not:** seeing heaven’s laws; not the Void Seeker’s hunt.

### 3 — Void qi (虚空灵气 · cultivated medium)

**Not a place** — a **kind of qi** refined inside the VR basin after breakthrough.

- Early VR ≈ DT Peak because you **do not have void qi yet** — only permission to cultivate it ([`realm-claims.md`](realm-claims.md)).
- Void qi **carries spatial property** — fuels passage arts, void techniques, arrays at VR tier.
- Faction flavor differs (Void Temple firmament-edge lineage vs Phoenix **Void Hollowing** — explicitly *not* Temple’s void).

### 4 — Firmament / veil (天幕 · sky shell)

Boundary of **the world as a game board** — below outer void, above mortal sky.

- Void Temple cosmology: shell around continent + seas ([`void-temple-sect.md`](void-temple-sect.md)).
- Most VR cultivators **press against** the veil from inside; they do not live in outer void.

### 5 — Outer void (外虚空)

Beyond the firmament. Void Seeker’s hunt. Firmament-edge qi. **Dao fracture** if mishandled.

- **Not** what a fresh VR patriarch does daily.
- Temple lineage trains **touching** outer-void qi without shattering — endgame sect fantasy.
- Void Prison is a **pocket** adjacent to this layer, not “the” outer void.

### 6 — Void pockets (虚空境 · engineered)

Artificial or semi-stable void-space: **Void Prison**, sealed sites, high arrays.

- **Void Temple** specialty — locks, gates, catalog.
- Uses void **principles**; not the same as personal void qi cultivation.

### 7 — Law-layer (法则层 · behind the veil)

**Not void at all** — what Peak VR **glimpses** when they see **past** the veil.

- Heaven’s rules **in action**: karma threading, tribulation forming, law expressing as phenomenon (fire as Phase, oaths as binding, etc.).
- You do not “walk” here — you **witness** it. Unauthorized witness triggers heaven’s response.

**Owner lean:** Peak VR fantasy = **see past the void/veil** = **glimpse the law-layer**. Colloquially “past the void” means **through firmament + outer void haze** to where rules move.

## Peak VR → Dao Seeking (owner lean 2026-08-02)

### The glimpse

At **Peak Void Refinement**, void work is no longer only “move through gaps.” The self is thin enough that, at the veil, you **catch heaven enforcing a rule** — laws **in action**, not a book on a shelf.

Examples (flavor, not mechanics):

- Watch tribulation **assemble** from karma threads.
- See a forbidden ground **thin** because two laws disagree.
- Witness Phase of Fire **express** bare at a caldera — not loot; **sight**.

### The tribulation — **Rulebook Retaliation** (working name)

Heaven does not hand out library cards. **Glimpsing** the law-layer is **trespass** — **not a test**. There is no fair audit; heaven strikes because you looked. **Survival depends on everything you prepared beforehand.**

| Beat | Fantasy |
|------|---------|
| **Trigger** | Peak VR consolidation / breakthrough attempt; veil parts for an instant |
| **Crisis** | Heaven **retaliates** — law made weapon (karma reversal, void collapse, identity scrub, “unwrite” your passage) |
| **Survive** | Stabilized sight; heaven cannot un-notice you → **Dao Seeking** |
| **Fail** | Dao fracture, blinded lens, regression, death — TBD |

**Solo journey (owner lock 2026-08-02):** No sect can sit the tribulation **for** you. Void Temple may help you **arrive ready**; they cannot share the retaliation.

**“Foundation” = full preparation**, not only FE qi foundation:

| Prep axis | Examples |
|-----------|----------|
| Cultivation foundation | Dantian, seal grade, void qi depth, VR peak quality |
| Arrays / formations | Shield arrays, karma deflection, void-anchor circles |
| Equipment | Treasures, talismans, relics tuned for tribulation or void |
| Aid consumed | Pills, one-shot protections — spent on the attempt |
| Karma / choices | Prior deeds may weight retaliation severity |

Copy lean: *“This is not a test. You trespassed. Be ready or do not look.”*

### Void Temple’s role (owner lock 2026-08-02)

Void Temple ≠ shortcut past retaliation. They **cultivate void more directly and efficiently** than generic jianghu VR paths.

| What Temple helps | What stays solo |
|-------------------|-----------------|
| **Void qi** refinement — firmament-edge lineage, gate arrays | **Glimpse** — you still peer alone |
| **DT → VR** breakthrough — higher **survival rate** (better prep, rites, void-anchor doctrine) | **VR → Seeking retaliation** — Temple prepares; **you** survive |
| Reading **firmament stress**, sealed sites, void-pocket maintenance | Wearing the consequence of heaven noticing you |

Temple disciples arrive at peak VR with **deeper void qi** and better tools — they do not skip Rulebook Retaliation; they **stack the deck** then walk in alone.

See [`void-temple-sect.md`](void-temple-sect.md).

**If you survive**, that **is** the Seeking power spike:

- **Lifespan** — moderate jump (~12–15k band entry; see [`nine-realm-ladder.md`](nine-realm-ladder.md))
- **Moderate power** — void qi + **law-sight** fused; patriarch-tier weight on the board
- **Comprehension access** — pursuits/contemplation open; library **deepens** over millennia

### Why this isn’t repeating VR’s empty early phase

| | VR entry | Seeking entry |
|--|----------|----------------|
| **Breakthrough** | Permission only (DT Peak) | **Retaliation survived** — real jump |
| **Grind** | Acquire void qi / arts | Deepen library, merges, threads |
| **Peak fantasy** | Glimpse law-layer | First **wield** → Manifestation |
