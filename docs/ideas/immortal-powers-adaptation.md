## Intent

Xianxia hands a Nascent Soul a divine ability and an Immortal a second physics engine: flight, clones, pocket worlds, time, fate, resurrection, a finger that erases a kingdom. Most of those verbs already have a home on the mortal ladder as **one claim**. The rest would delete the loops this game is actually about — travel, the clock, tribulation, civic seats, the economy, and death.

An immortal power should feel like **authority with a witness**. Someone, some array, or heaven’s clause can still answer. The wonder is the fiction and the consequence, not a coefficient.

Parents: [`realm-claims.md`](realm-claims.md), [`immortal-world-layer.md`](immortal-world-layer.md), [`dao-seeking-and-manifestation.md`](dao-seeking-and-manifestation.md), [`post-immortal-cosmology.md`](post-immortal-cosmology.md). This doc does not add a spellbook on top of those.

## Design notes

### What the genre typically unlocks

Novels do not wait for “Immortal” to get flashy. The famous list arrives in bands. Names vary (化神, 返虚, 合体, 大乘, 真仙); the toys are stable.

| Band in fiction | Typical 神通 | Feel |
|-----------------|--------------|------|
| Qi / Foundation | Qi sense, elemental arts, a clumsy light body | You left mortality |
| Golden Core | Sword-flight, a domain seed, divine sense measured in *li* | You occupy space |
| Nascent Soul | Soul leaves the corpse, a life lamp, the first real **divine ability**, a weak clone | You are no longer one body |
| Deity / Void | Law-seed, regional presence, teleport, the seed of a grotto | You touch the rules |
| Dao / Mahayana | One law worn on the body; heaven starts keeping a file | You *are* a rule, locally |
| Immortal and above | Grotto-heaven, immortal spells that ignore mortal arts, fate eyes, create life, time, unmake a region | You edit the board |

“Immortal powers” in the owner’s question are that last row, plus the Nascent Soul row that readers remember as the moment combat stopped being techniques and became mythology.

### Why the raw list breaks this game

| If the player can… | The loop that dies |
|--------------------|--------------------|
| Fly, blink, or skip with no ceiling | [`world-scale-and-travel.md`](world-scale-and-travel.md) — distance is the map |
| Dilate time, rewind, or run nine seclusions at once | Living clock, lifespan as tragedy, sect ecology |
| Spawn a clone that cultivates or holds a seat | One body, one calendar, one civic actor |
| Read every secret, realm, and ending | Spiritual sense limits, mystery, broken-core tells |
| Compel a city to agree | Conversation, loyalty, seats |
| Revive from a drop of blood, or raise the dead for free | Tribulation, grudges, succession |
| Conjure pills, weapons, or years | Economy, professions, veins |
| Erase a zone with one art | The board itself |

Bigger damage numbers are the small version of the same bug. An immortal fighting a Qi Condensation cultivator is not a DPS check. They should never reach a fair exchange. An immortal fighting a peer is politics, formations, and law — still not a button that deletes Dustbone.

### The adaptation rule

**A power is allowed when it opens a new verb at a bounded scope, and something in the world can still say no.**

Three brakes, used together:

| Brake | Means | Novel habit it replaces |
|-------|--------|-------------------------|
| **Scope** | One zone, one law, one named target, one term. Not the continent, not “whenever.” | “My domain is the world” |
| **Contest** | A peer, an array, a basin statute, heaven’s clause, or the target’s own realm can dampen, refuse, or price it | Weaker beings auto-lose and the power is the whole story |
| **Bill** | Calendar (a Work), heaven’s attention, a Mandate spike, or the one Taboo of that life | Free, instant, repeatable |

Cool specifically means three more things:

1. **The law you wear colors the verb.** There is no shared immortal spell list. A sword immortal and a yin immortal both “suppress a disaster,” and the chronicle should not be able to mix them up.
2. **Scarce slots, not a bar of icons.** Choosing which blessing, which Work, which once-in-a-life Taboo is the build. Unlocking the whole list on the afternoon of Ascension is the break.
3. **The power makes a scene someone else can enter.** An edict is visible on the zone. A scar stays on the map. A rival ascendant answers in the scheduler. If nobody can react, it was a cheat code.

Cadence is already designed in [`immortal-world-layer.md`](immortal-world-layer.md). This doc hangs tropes on it:

| Cadence | Reversible? | What it is for |
|---------|-------------|----------------|
| **Edict** | Mostly | The weekly immortal verb. Small, regional, answerable |
| **Work** | Hard | The multi-decade project. Where grottoes, veins, and seals live |
| **Taboo** | Never | One per life. The finger-of-god, the fate rewrite, the raising of the dead — pick at most one story |

Do not show all four power tiers (Influence, Legislation, Domain creation, Destruction) on day one. Court standing and dao comprehension open them, same as that doc.

### Already spent — do not grant them again at Immortal

From [`realm-claims.md`](realm-claims.md):

| Idx | Claim | Genre toy it already is |
|-----|--------|-------------------------|
| 0 | Perception | Mortal qi-feel. Not omniscience |
| 1 | Anchor | Rooted; intent; probe a seal |
| 2 | Domain | Pressure, “my ground,” **Light Body** in-zone |
| 3 | Sovereignty | Soul sense, light projection, **Sky Travel** between zones |
| 4 | Transformation | Deity-presence; NPCs defer. Not a giant hitbox |
| 5 | Passage | **Blink** between local nodes. Not map-wide teleport |
| 6 | Law (seek) | Read laws. Script-sight of *pressure*, not spoilers |
| 7 | Law (wear) | One worn law. Local imposition. Tribulation takes its color |
| 8 | Transcendence | Leave the walk loop. The immortal fork |

Flight is finished. Golden Core glides inside a zone, Nascent Soul crosses the continent, Void blinks a road, Immortal stops walking as the main verb. A fourth speed tier is not an immortal power.

Spiritual sense stays the doc in [`spiritual-sense-cultivation-reading.md`](spiritual-sense-cultivation-reading.md): delta versus the target, misreads, offence. Immortal sense is **hearing your own jurisdiction** — something disturbed the edict, the vein, the seal — not a radar of every cultivator in Dustbone.

### Trope by trope

#### Divine sense, soul attack, possession

Fiction: sweep a province, pull out secrets, crush any weaker soul, wear their body.

Adaptation: sense stays comparative. Soul force against a peer is a fight. Against a mortal it is the deference Golden Core and Deity Transformation already buy — they fold, stall, leak, and remember. They do not become puppets.

**Possession is refused** as a player verb. It throws away the body, the path, and the seat you spent the life building. Demonic NPCs may do it as horror. A player who wants that fantasy is on a path that **ends** the current life, not a combat stance.

#### Clones and avatars (分身)

The sharpest action-economy break in the genre. Nine bodies cultivating is nine clocks.

| Version | Verdict |
|---------|---------|
| A second body with its own cultivate / travel / seat queue | **Refuse** |
| A combat double that exists for one exchange and dies with it | Flourish of the worn law. No loot, no seat, no seclusion |
| **Incarnation visit** — one body walks the mortal map | Allowed only if the Court clock is the same clock. While you are in the street you are not also legislating. Open in the immortal-world doc; this is the cap if we say yes |
| Life lamp / spare nascent | One prepared anchor, a Work, and you return **diminished** (realm notch or a Mandate spike). Not “I always have a spare” |

#### Grotto-heaven (洞天)

Fiction: a private dimension, herbs, and time at a hundred to one, hidden from heaven.

The borrowed-law pocket is already the **Domain creation** tier: you become a rival legislator, and heaven notices. Keep that.

| Grotto promise | Adaptation |
|----------------|------------|
| A place that is yours | A cultivation site you authored. Yields on the **world** clock. It does not outpace the best mortal vein by being “inside you” |
| Time dilation | **Refuse** past the seclusion presets the chronicle already uses. A cave that outruns the living clock makes every other lifespan a joke |
| Hidden from the jianghu | Yes — mortals and sects lose the scent |
| Hidden from heaven | No, while you are a Mandate node. Rule 7 still bills the world. Unregistering is Chaos, not a furniture upgrade |

#### Law domain

Fiction: the battlefield becomes your element; later, the province does.

Mortal version is Dao Manifestation: **one** worn law, local, swappable with a cost. Immortal version is an **Edict** or a **Work** that emphasizes or weakens that same law in **one region**. Heaven resists. You do not wear five laws because you ascended. Tribulation weather in that region skews. Weak techniques cough. A peer wearing the contrary law is a real answer, not a cutscene loss.

#### Time

Stop, rewind, accelerate a war, age a saint to dust. This one deletes tribulation, travel, and the clock in a single art.

| Version | Verdict |
|---------|---------|
| Spend years on a closed project while the world also advances | Already seclusion / Works. The sanctioned skip |
| Tribulation arrives in the color of your law | Already Manifestation. It does not skip the gate |
| Shave years off a **mortal** in one fight | A technique with a ceiling. A peer has more years than you can tax |
| Rewind a death, a choice, or a day | **Refuse.** The chronicle only runs forward |
| Freeze a battlefield long enough to take extra actions | **Refuse.** That is a second action queue |

#### Fate, karma, eyes

Fiction: see the ending, rewrite a destiny, know the hidden genius.

Seeking may already grow a **script-sight of pressure**: this seat is thinning, this vein is sick, this tribulation will not be ordinary. That is an omen. It has false branches. It is not a plot spoiler and it is not an edit.

**Rewriting a named fate** is a Taboo. One per life. The world remembers, the scheduler answers, and you do not get a menu of redos. Reading “who wins the war” is refused even as a Taboo — it ends stories instead of making one.

#### Resurrection

| Version | Verdict |
|---------|---------|
| You no longer age | The Mandate lease. It is a bill on the world, not proof you transcended. See post-immortal cosmology |
| You die and stand up at full strength | **Refuse.** Violent death from a peer, a calamity, or a heaven clause stays real |
| One soul lamp | A Work spent in advance. One charge. Return diminished. Tribulation failure still hurts |
| Stall one mortal’s death for a term of years | An Edict-scale blessing. You did not grant immortality |
| Raise the dead as themselves | **Refuse** on the ordered path. A Taboo may raise an **echo** — useful, wrong, and remembered. True recall of a person is Chaos or a story we have not earned |

#### The finger that destroys a kingdom

Combat stays comparative and usually refuses to start. A Qi Condensation cultivator does not get a duel with you; your presence already ended it. A peer meets you in law, arrays, and calendar.

**Destruction** in the immortal layer is aimed at a **binding**, not at geography: sever one law’s hold, unmake one **registered** pocket. The map is the board. Deleting Dustbone ends the game.

The one exception is a Taboo that **scars a place** the way the Sunless Scar scars Frostbite: permanent, named, and every sect plus heaven treats it as an event. Once in a life. Never a basic attack.

#### Creation from nothing

Conjured pills, natal weapons, and newborn spirits skip the economy.

Ordered immortals **shape what is already there**: move a vein, ripen a field’s qi, temper an armament, shift a tribulation’s weather. Inputs remain world inputs.

**True creation** — an unregistered life, a weapon heaven has no clause for — stays Chaos-only, as [`immortal-world-layer.md`](immortal-world-layer.md) already locks.

#### Giant bodies, beast forms, “I become the mountain”

Presentation of the worn law or the body path, inside one scene. Civic pressure does not scale with fictional height. Deity Transformation already makes a city defer. You do not gain a city-wide area attack because the prose said “a hundred zhang.”

#### Sealing

This is the immortal verb that matches the owner’s taste (finishing *I Shall Seal the Heavens* because the climax was a sealing). Prefer seals over blasts.

| Seal | Scope | Contest |
|------|--------|---------|
| A person | A prison, not a deletion. They can be unsealed | A peer, their sect, a later Work |
| A site | The mouth of a forbidden ground stays shut for an era. The node remains | Arrays, a contrary edict, the site’s own axis |
| A clause of heaven | Endgame. Chaos / Progenitor. Not the first immortal toy | Heaven’s default denial, then the whole upstairs |

#### Blessings and curses

An edict: bless one zone’s qi, suppress one disaster, thin one region’s recruitment for a **term**. Visible on the zone card. Reversible.

A curse that auto-collapses a faction is refused. Friction is the curse — thin qi, ugly tribulations, disciples who stop coming. The target answers with patronage, arrays, or their own immortal.

#### Mind control and charm

**Refuse.** Conversation, loyalty, and seats are the social game. “They agree because you are heavy” is domain and deity presence: they still want things, and they plot after you leave. A compulsion art, if a demonic manual ever has one, needs backlash and a target who can be too strong to hold. It never means “the city is mine.”

#### Extra dantians, extra souls

**Refuse** for the player. One chamber, one path, one worn law. A boss may have a second heart as a fight gimmick. The player does not get a second cultivation track by ascending.

#### Immortal combat arts

Allowed as **expressions of the worn law** with the same budget as a mortal art: one strong option, a counter exists, preparation matters. The log should sound like mythology. The numbers should not skip the fight against anyone who is actually allowed to fight you.

### What an ordered immortal’s kit actually is

Not a new bar of spells. Four tiers, already named, now filled with the tropes they are allowed to satisfy:

| Tier | Player fantasy | Tropes it is allowed to be | Tropes it is not |
|------|----------------|----------------------------|------------------|
| **Influence** | The region can feel your attention | Bless a qi tide, wake a sealed site, hold off one disaster, stall one death | Create life, grant realms, mind control |
| **Legislation** | A law leans because you said so | Emphasize or weaken **your** law in one region; tribulation weather changes | Time stop, five laws at once, global domain |
| **Domain creation** | You authored a small heaven | Borrowed-law pocket, a spirit vein, a seal on a site | Time dilation, hiding from the Mandate, true creation |
| **Destruction** | You remove a rule, not a place | Sever one binding, unmake one registered pocket | Delete a zone, finger-of-god as a basic art |
| **Taboo** (one) | The world will not forget | Scar a place, rewrite one fate, raise one echo, a heaven-wounding act you will pay for | A second taboo, a silent one, an undo |

Ascent itself gives the **fork** (linger and legislate, or reincarnate), the end of mortal travel as the main loop, and the horrible quiet of the lease — borrowed stillness, effortless qi where the pipes are fat, thin qi in the backwater. That feeling is the first “power.” The toolkit opens after, with standing.

### Half-Step — a glimpse, not the kit

[`nine-realm-ladder.md`](nine-realm-ladder.md) left Half-Step powers open. Half-Step is still Dao Manifestation. It should not grow a flight tier, a clone, or a pocket.

**Proposal: Lease-glimpse.**

You are far enough that heaven’s file has a blank line with your name. Once, at mortal scale, you may push the worn law **one step past** its normal imposition — a single site, or a single day of edict-like weather — and heaven answers in the same breath (attention, a scar on the tribulation you have not taken yet, a dream of ledger-threads). You learn that Ascension will **enroll** you. You do not receive Court verbs, free travel, or a second action.

If that still feels like a mechanic smuggled in early, the fallback is smaller: Half-Step is only the gate and the omen. No new verb until the breakthrough. Travel and law stay at Manifestation peak.

Either way, Half-Step is not a tenth realm and not a spell rank.

### Chaos and True Immortal

They may break **one** brake on purpose. That is the path.

| State | Which brake they earn | What they still do not get |
|-------|----------------------|----------------------------|
| **True Immortal** | The Mandate bill. Self-sustenance, so the world stops paying your lifespan | Clones, time rewind, zone deletion, mind control |
| **Chaos / Unwritten** | The registry. You may author what heaven has no clause for (true creation, a law that does not eat the young) | Admin mode. One authorship, contested by heaven’s denial behaviors |
| **Progenitor** | You write ledgers | Still one work of authorship the player can actually play, not every refused trope at once |

Ascending on the ordered path does not unlock these. It unlocks the bill.
