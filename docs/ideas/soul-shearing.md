# Soul shearing

Updated: 2026-11-28. Source: author design discussion. Accepted direction, provisional procedure; no implementation.

## Intent and constraints

A reckless or exceptionally prepared cultivator may divide their existing soul into two or more structurally sound souls. Unlike the [Twin Aspect Soul](innate-gifts.md), this redistributes existing strength: pouring water from one cup into another versus starting with two full cups. Resulting souls initially have less strength and can independently develop if successfully stabilised.

This must remain a viable but niche practice, substantially worse than natural twin souls, not a routine optimisation. Tampering with the soul is dangerous, can have unwanted effects and should attract some heavenly opposition. Exact heavenly response is unresolved. Destruction of a sheared aspect is permanent and should inflict significant damage on the primary.

## Provisional procedure and identity

Separation, structural stabilisation and identity synchronisation are useful conceptual stages, not a locked gameplay sequence. A newly separated soul may lack a coherent shared identity unless supplied with memories and time to integrate; identity divergence is a possible risk. A lengthy synchronisation period may suffice for ordinary successful procedures. No mandatory rebellion or personality simulation is approved.

Potential benefits include independent cultivation, simultaneous techniques and more than two souls. Exact requirements, costs, limits, failure outcomes, resource allocation, primary loss and heavenly consequences remain open. Do not assume automatic immortality or a standard route to extra lives.

The [Soul King](war-legends.md) is a historical extreme, not a benchmark for ordinary shearing. His ninefold simultaneous division is closer to total disassembly and reconstruction than routine separation.
