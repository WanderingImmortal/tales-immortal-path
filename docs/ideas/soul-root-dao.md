## Intent

**Root Dao (根道)** is the spirit lane’s **purview layer** — exactly **five** branches, not the same shelf as Art Great Daos (Sword, Poison, …). A spirit-primary cultivator **walks one or two branches** to idx 6–7.

**Owner lock (2026-10-13):** **Inheritance** = **common, crude grasp** of the branch — you *have* the power but route it **roughly** (waste Focus, bleed scope, unfocused). **Manuals (arts)** = **condensed routing** — how to converge that power into effective illusions, reads, thread-weaves, etc. The **most devastating** expressions of each branch are **art-gated**, not free on the inherit ladder. See § Crude inherit vs art.

This doc ties **nine-realm spirit names** ([`nine-realm-ladder.md`](nine-realm-ladder.md)) to **when branches open** and **how strong inherited skills should feel** vs generic `soul_condensation` ([`spirit-path-full-design.md`](spirit-path-full-design.md)).

**Inherited skills:** formal **expression tiers** (below) — more **crude slots** over the ladder, not polished peak kits. **Greater** = second crude grasp tier, not a finished sect art.

**Five branches (locked for now):** Karma · Fate · Memory · Illusion · **True Self** (真我 — Personal God home; not “Ego Dao” in UI).

**Parity (owner 2026-10-13):** spirit is **not** mainly “win the duel.” People fear soul cultivators who **studied the wrong scrolls** — each Root Dao at its pinnacle is a worthy threat ([§ Branch apex fiction](#branch-apex-fiction-owner-lock-2026-10-13)), but players reach that through **arts**, not inherit alone. Crude inherit stays **fight-fair**; **focused + devastating** = manuals. See § Society menace.

## Crude inherit vs art (owner lock 2026-10-13)

### Fiction

Root Dao gives **raw purview** — like **light**. Without an art, a soul cultivator **splashes** it: misread leaks, reads snag only surface, illusions cost double and flicker. An **art (manual)** is a **lens**: same light, **converged** — precise target, proper duration, real bite. For **AoE** or **chronicle** effects, the art is a **pattern** (array, scripture chapter) that routes power across many threads without treating each as a separate sniper shot — still “focused,” but **spread by design**, not by accident.

### What inheritance grants

| Grant | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Branch unlock** | You may **comprehend** arts of that `rootBranch` |
| **Lesser / greater / supreme inherit** | **Crude** techniques — playable, identity-clear, **inefficient** |
| **Statute (idx 7)** | **Worn soul-law** — passive routing, credibility, **comprehend tier** unlock; **not** the apex devastation button |

Crude markers (implementation leans — tune in playtest): **+Focus cost**, **−duration / −chance**, **narrower or random side-effect**, **strain on supreme crude**.

### What manuals grant

| Manual band | Meaning |
|-------------|---------|
| **Common–rare arts** | **Focused** versions of lesser/greater themes (replace or upgrade crude on shelf) |
| **High / forbidden arts** | **Devastating** branch expressions ([§ Branch apex fiction](#branch-apex-fiction-owner-lock-2026-10-13)) — puppet format, thread massacre, fortune shear, heart-demon manifest |
| **Sets & lineage** | Same lens, **grade** bumps (peerless = tightest beam) |

**Comprehend gates (lean):** need branch unlock + inherit tier ≥ manual tier (e.g. apex art requires supreme crude or statute seat).

### Why both exist

- **Inherit:** no scroll runner is helpless; branch fantasy readable from realm 1.
- **Manual:** reason to join sects, rob tombs, pursue jianghu — **power efficiency and horror** live here.
- **Balance:** devastators are **loot/commitment** gated; crude keeps basin PvE honest.

## Society menace vs combat kit

### Fantasy (why NPCs flinch)

| Fear | Branch home | When it shows |
|------|-------------|----------------|
| “They **know** what I hid” | **Memory** | Reads, ledger marks, tribunal confession |
| “I can’t trust my **eyes** or witnesses” | **Illusion** | False presence, forged scene *(manual)* |
| “Old debts / blood feuds might **wake**” | **Karma** | Thread tug, feud recall, stain amplification *(upper + manuals)* |
| “Our clan’s **luck** could turn” | **Fate** | Nudge outcomes, omen, marriage/exam bias *(upper + manuals)* |
| “They could **remake** or **bind** me” | **Memory** apex *(+ stain)* | Life-format / puppet — not idx 2 innates |
| “Blood washes blood **by itself**” | **Karma** apex | Thread-weave feuds |
| “The **sect’s luck** died” | **Fate** apex | Area / institution fortune collapse |

Threat tone: **insidious** — unknowable **future** harm, not only HP today. Fits [`heaven-cycle-and-apexes.md`](heaven-cycle-and-apexes.md) soul stigma and Void Temple watching soul tampering.

### Two channels (design rule)

```text
  INHERIT (crude grasp) ──► branch unlock + rough lesser → greater → supreme
       │                      fight-fair, wasteful, unfocused
       ▼
  ARTS (manuals) ──► focused routing at each band + DEVASTATING apex scrolls
       │                      comprehend gates; sect / black market / tomb
       ▼
  STATUTE (idx 7) ──► soul-law seat — boosts arts of primary branch; passive clause
       ▼
  STAIN / SACRILEGE ──► worst arts + mass use — ledger — [`alignment-sacrilege-corruption.md`](alignment-sacrilege-corruption.md)
```

**Owner lock (2026-10-13):** apex **fiction** belongs to each branch; apex **gameplay** is **art-gated** ([§ Crude inherit vs art](#crude-inherit-vs-art-owner-lock-2026-10-13)). Bounded clauses, not menu wipe.

Full peak fiction: [§ Branch apex fiction](#branch-apex-fiction-owner-lock-2026-10-13). Crude inherit **tilts** by branch; **focused / devastating** = manuals + statute as **enabler**.

## Branch apex fiction (owner lock 2026-10-13)

**Rule:** anything taken to the **pinnacle** of a Root Dao is a **worthy threat** — xianxia scale. **Fiction** lives here; **playable devastation** is **`manual` apex arts** (primary branch + statute seat helps). **Crude supreme inherit** = you can **attempt** a weak, costly echo of the theme — not the sect-killing art. **Balance** = constraints below + art rarity, not weak lore.

### Shared constraints (how peak stays playable)

| Lever | Use |
|-------|-----|
| **Scope** | One foe, one scene, one clan thread, one “real rule” at a time — no instant map wipe |
| **Cost** | Focus drain, **soul strain**, lifespan, rare materials, months-long rite for worst outcomes |
| **Telegraph** | NPCs and UI show **omen** / **Known** / **thread tug** before payoff lands |
| **Counters** | Equal-realm **True Self**, Memory reader, clarity wards, Void Temple attention, tribulation |
| **Chronicle** | Empire/sect fall = **situation-thread cascade** ([`jianghu-situation-threads.md`](jianghu-situation-threads.md)), not one click |
| **Stain** | Mass puppeting, forced mass killing, cycle tampering → **sacrilege** escalates future gates |
| **Manuals** | **Required** for focused power; **only** source of **devastating** apex arts |
| **Crude inherit** | Baseline only — never outperforms a matched-grade art on same theme |

**Inheritance tilt:** Illusion & Memory peak **can** include terrifying **combat-real** outcomes; Karma & Fate peak skew **jianghu / institution**; True Self TBD but likely **anti-domination** + godhood runway.

### Illusion (幻) — *假作真*

**Peak fiction:** an illusionist at the summit can make **real**, for purposes of harming you, **essentially anything they can hold in mind** — weapon, terrain, beast, calamity — not cartoon omnipotence: **one declared form**, **one bounded law** (“this bite counts,” “this wall blocks”).

| Tier | Expression |
|------|------------|
| **Crude supreme inherit** | **Weight of the Unreal** — fuzzy form, **one weak real rule**, high waste |
| **Statute** | **The Lie Holds** — **seat**: illusions **hold longer**; **comprehend** high illusion arts; passive credibility |
| **Apex arts (manual)** | e.g. **Heart-Demon Manifestation** — **think it, it bites** (focused, strain, cooldown) |
| **Other manuals** | Snare scriptures, false witness, multi-foe haze — focused lesser/greater |

**Counters:** Memory **truth read**, True Self anchor, “too absurd for realm” hard cap, strain if form exceeds mass/realm budget.

### Memory (忆) — *改忆塑命*

**Peak fiction:** a memory dao apex can **rewrite the narrative of a life** — format identity, loyalties, remembered oaths — until the person is a **perfectly obedient puppet** (genre: soul formatting / 夺忆).

| Tier | Expression |
|------|------------|
| **Crude supreme inherit** | **Vault Unsealing** — one **sealed layer** pull, costly, often incomplete |
| **Statute** | **The Record Endures** / **Life Ledger** seat — **Known** mark; enables **comprehend** memory apex arts |
| **Apex arts (manual)** | e.g. **Life Format Scripture** — puppet / rewrite (rite, stain, contest) |
| **Other manuals** | Confession seal, witness rubbing — focused reads/edits |

**Constraints (mandatory):** **time + rite**, target will/True Self contest, **heavy stain** on full puppet, Void Temple / heaven notice, **reversible** only at extreme cost (genre rare). **Not** instant turn-1 dominate in duel — **chronicle horror**.

**Counters:** True Self primary, supreme anchor, grotto wards, killing the formatter mid-rite.

### Karma (因果) — *织业杀机*

**Peak fiction:** weave **karma threads** so people **organically** ruin each other — blood feuds wake, cousins “remember,” guards look away — **not** mind control; the world **finds reasons**. Full design: [§ Karma branch](#branch-focus--karma-因果).

| Tier | Expression |
|------|------------|
| **Crude inherit** | Thread Sense, Crude Reinforce, Thread Fracture — see / tighten / loosen |
| **Statute** | **Debts Come Due** — seat; marks **couple** over time |
| **Apex arts (manual)** | **Blood Debt Orchestration** — situation-thread cascade |

### Fate (命) — *气数倾覆*

**Peak fiction:** manipulate **fortune of place and institution** — sect examination luck, vein yield, patron attention — until **empires or sects fall** “naturally” (misfortune cascade).

| Tier | Expression (stubs — detail on Fate pass) |
|------|------------------------------------------|
| **Lesser / greater** | Thread Glimpse, Nudge — telegraph, small bias |
| **Crude supreme inherit** | **Fortune Shear** *(working)* — minor luck dip, one thread nudge |
| **Statute** | **Omen Crown** *(working)* — seat; omen passives in claim |
| **Apex arts (manual)** | e.g. **Sect Fortune Collapse** — institution cascade (chronicle) |

**Other manuals:** Clan omen pin, marriage thread — focused fate bias.

**Counters:** fate-rite tribulation, heavenly luck treasures, Immortal-level counter-fate (late).

## Branch unlock stagger

Branches unlock on **spirit track realm idx**, not qi realm. Dabblers on another primary track do **not** get the full innate ladder (see § Dabblers).

| Spirit idx | Realm (short) | Branch unlocked | Lesser inherited (auto) | Greater inherited (auto) |
|------------|---------------|-----------------|-------------------------|----------------------------|
| **1** | Sea Stilling | **Illusion** | **Misdirecting Gaze** — debuff **one foe** (see § Illusion) | @ **2** **Hollow Double** — **self** decoy echo |
| **2** | Soul Seed | **Memory** | **Surface Inscription** — **foe** surface read (see § Memory) | @ **3** **Soul Search** — deep read *(canon migrate)* |
| **3** | Soul Birth | **Karma** | **Thread Sense** (§ Karma) | @ **4** **Crude Reinforce** |
| **4** | Soul Transformation | **Fate** | **Thread Glimpse** — see next intent / telegraph one action | @ **5** **Nudge** — small action-order or proc bias *(not hard stun)* |
| **5** | Ego Refinement | **True Self** | **Self Anchor** — resist possession / illusion peel | @ **6** **Name Bound** — declare self; buff vs identity attacks |
| **6** | Divinity Seeking | *(no sixth branch)* | **Crude supreme** for **primary** branch + clause draft UI | Apex **arts** still manual |
| **7** | Divine Soul | — | **Statute** (primary branch) | Pseudo God peak abilities |

**Commit rule (lean):** at **Soul Birth (idx 3)** pick **one primary Root branch** for idx 7 statute; optional **secondary** at idx 5 with reduced greater-cap. Changing branch = rare rite (same family as meridian-wash — [`cultivation-manuals-framework.md`](cultivation-manuals-framework.md)).

**High-tier-only branch *curves* (not absence):** Karma and Fate **lessers are info/setup**; payoffs land on greater/supreme. Illusion **lesser/greater are survival control** in QC–GC; **“illusion becomes real”** is **supreme + statute**, never lesser. Memory **lessers are strong in QC–FE band**. True Self is **mostly idx 5+**.

## Branch focus — Illusion (幻)

**Fantasy:** you do not out-tank; you **spoof what the enemy thinks is true** — first about **you**, then about **the field**. Xianxia endgame: **假作真** — a lie heavy enough to **bite**; that belongs at **supreme/statute**, not Sea Stilling.

### Targeting rule (UI)

Every inherited illusion art names **who is affected**:

| Target tag | Means |
|------------|--------|
| **Foe** | One enemy spirit / mind — debuff or mis-aim |
| **Self** | Your soul presentation — decoy, misread profile, guard fudge |
| **Field** | Zone illusion — supreme+ only in v1 (costly, short) |

### Crude inherit ladder (Illusion-primary)

All rows = **unfocused** usage unless player learns a matching **art**. Example focused manual in *italics*.

| Tier | Spirit idx | Name (working) | Target | Effect (design) | Power note |
|------|------------|----------------|--------|-----------------|------------|
| **Lesser** | 1 | **Misdirecting Gaze** | **Foe** | **Crude Misread:** scattershot — next hostile action vs you **may** miss; extra Focus vs a focused snare art. | *Manual e.g. **Empty-Eye Snare** — tight single-target Misread* |
| **Greater** | 2 | **Hollow Double** | **Self** | **Crude decoy:** flickering echo, **one** hit soak, high leak cost. | *Manual e.g. **Soul Afterimage Art** — stable decoy + step* |
| **Supreme** | 5–6 *(primary)* | **Weight of the Unreal** | **Field** | **Crude realification:** vague form, one **weak** real rule, heavy waste. | *Apex manual e.g. **Heart-Demon Manifestation** — think it, it bites* |
| **Statute** | 7 | **The Lie Holds** | Seat | Credibility + comprehend high illusion arts; **not** a free apex cast. | See [§ Illusion apex](#illusion-幻--假作真) |

**Name swaps open:** Misdirecting Gaze → **Mind Snare**, **False Opening**; Hollow Double → **Soul Afterimage**, **Borrowed Body**.

### Why this fits squishy early spirit

Idx 1–2 you **kite**: Misdirecting Gaze on the scariest striker, Hollow Double when focused. Damage still comes from **generic** Soul Lash / manuals until GC+. Branch does **not** give basin burst.

### Manuals (Illusion)

**Focused combat/jianghu:** replace or upgrade crude slots — `rootBranch: illusion`, tier ≤ inherit tier to comprehend.

**Apex / devastating (manual only):** **Heart-Demon Manifestation**, **False Presence** (scene-as-real in jianghu), spread-pattern haze scriptures — require supreme crude or statute seat.

## Branch focus — Memory (忆)

**Fantasy:** Memory does **not** paint lies (Illusion) — it **reads what was inscribed**: recent intent, hidden loadout, sealed past. Pre-birth **Soul Seed** is the sea **condensing a trace**; Memory unlocks there because recall becomes a **weapon**.

**Vs Illusion:** Illusion edits **presentation**; Memory extracts **record**. Illusion supreme makes a fake **bite once**; Memory supreme **pulls a sealed truth** (identity, technique signature, quest shard).

### Targeting rule (UI)

| Target tag | Means |
|------------|--------|
| **Foe (surface)** | One enemy — **recent / shallow** layer only |
| **Foe (deep)** | One enemy or NPC — **full Soul Search** depth |
| **Record** | A **marked** mind or chronicle hook — supreme+ (pull sealed layer) |

Out of combat: surface = **lie hint / aura tell**; deep = dialogue and explore gates ([`spiritual-sense-cultivation-reading.md`](spiritual-sense-cultivation-reading.md), [`disguise-and-public-identity.md`](disguise-and-public-identity.md)).

### Crude inherit ladder (Memory-primary)

| Tier | Spirit idx | Name (working) | Target | Effect (design) | Power note |
|------|------------|----------------|--------|-----------------|------------|
| **Lesser** | 2 | **Surface Inscription** | **Foe (surface)** | **Crude read** — snag **one** shallow tell or lie hint; may **fail partial** vs wards. | *Manual e.g. **Recent Trace Scripture** — reliable surface read* |
| **Greater** | 3 | **Soul Search** | **Foe (deep)** | **Crude deep probe** — grant via Memory branch (migrate from flat pool). Noisier / costlier than focused search art. | *Manual e.g. **Palace of Mirrors Search** — full utility* |
| **Supreme** | 5–6 *(primary)* | **Vault Unsealing** | **Record** | **Crude** seal pull — incomplete layers, backlash. | *Apex manual e.g. **Life Format Scripture** — puppet / rewrite* |
| **Statute** | 7 | **The Record Endures** / **Life Ledger** | Seat | **Known** mark; enables apex memory **comprehend**; no free Format. | [§ Memory apex](#memory-忆--改忆塑命) |

### Pairing with Illusion (idx 1–3 dabble)

| Combo | Role |
|-------|------|
| **Misdirecting Gaze → Surface Inscription** | They mis-aim while you **read** whether they had a hidden opener |
| **Soul Search after fight** | Intel path — less burst, wins **lie / hidden intent** gates |

**Focused:** confession seal, witness rubbing, multi-foe surface — upgrade crude slots.

## Branch focus — Karma (因果)

**Fantasy:** Karma is **invisible thread** between people — debts, favors, blood, sect, marriage. Walkers **see**, **reinforce**, or **sever** couplings; they do not usually **spawn** a thick thread from nothing (help someone → **real** incident → **then** reinforce). OP fiction = everyone’s threads tug at once; **playable** = tiered sight, capped reinforce, delayed beats ([`jianghu-situation-threads.md`](jianghu-situation-threads.md)).

### Not the same as qi **Karma Dao**

| | **Root Karma (spirit)** | **Karma Dao (qi Seeking)** |
|--|-------------------------|----------------------------|
| Shelf | Soul Root branch — [`soul-root-dao.md`](soul-root-dao.md) | Art Great Dao — [`data.js`](../../data.js) `karma`, `karma_debt`, `karma_grace` |
| Job | **Manipulate couplings** between actors (incidents → threads) | **Comprehend** consequence; seer hints, alignment read, tribulation insight |
| Code today | *(not shipped)* | `hasKarmaDaoAccess()`, `performKarmaSeerReading()` in [`dao-taxonomy.js`](../../dao-taxonomy.js) |
| Dual walker | Spirit statute + qi Karma fragments = reader **and** weaver — tune stain |

**Dao alignment** ([`alignment-sacrilege-corruption.md`](alignment-sacrilege-corruption.md)) = philosophy / NPC reactions — **not** heaven’s ledger. **Corruption / sacrilege** = abusing cycle. Karma arts **read and tug** interpersonal **incidents**; forcing blood on the **clean** adds **corruption** on the **walker**, not “karma points on victim.”

### Balance core (owner 2026-10-13)

**Rule:** Karma power scales with **real recorded cause**, not caster level alone.

| Principle | Implementation lean |
|-----------|---------------------|
| **No thick thread from vacuum** | **Reinforce** needs an **existing edge** (any tier) or **fresh incident** you caused; **sever** cuts what’s there |
| **Organic, not dominate** | Outcomes = **situation-thread beats** (rumor, ambush, denunciation, duel challenge) — not “target attacks ally this turn” in combat UI |
| **Delayed** | Marks **charge**; apex arts **schedule** beats via [`world-scheduler.js`](../../world-scheduler.js) spine |
| **Escapable** | Merit, apology, mediator NPC, cutting mark, killing weaver mid-rite |
| **Boomerang** | Forcing feud between innocents → **corruption** + Void Temple attention; misfire if target **True Self** / charter protected |
| **Combat role** | **Setup and punish the stained** — small damage amp vs **oath-broken / marked** foes; not a second damage school |

```text
  INCIDENT ── creates / strengthens an edge (gameplay: help, betray, kill → incident ledger)
       │
  THREAD SENSE ── see edges (tiered; usually strongest-first)
       │
  REINFORCE ── +heat / +grudgeLevel / ally “linger” bias on THAT edge
       │
  SEVER (late, manual-heavy) ── cut edges → isolation, weak retaliation web
       │
  BEAT ── situation-thread engine (organic, not dominate)
```

### Thread model (design data — aligns with situation threads)

A **karma thread** is an **edge** in the social graph (not necessarily a full active `situationThread` yet):

| Field (lean) | Purpose |
|--------------|---------|
| `a`, `b` | Two uids (person↔person, person↔org, org↔org) |
| `kind` | `debt` · `grudge` · `favor` · `blood` · `oath` · `sect` · `marriage` · `patron` |
| **Strength** | 1–5 — derived from incident **severity**, age, repetition, public witness |
| **Heat** | 0–100 — volatility inside strength (reinforce adds heat; time decays) |
| `situationThreadId` | Optional — once edge escalates to active pack |

**Creating ties:** normal play (**help, save, gift, oath**) writes **incidents** → edges appear. Karma **reinforce** amplifies; **raw “create thread”** arts (if any) only mint **strength-1 faint** edges that still need reinforcement / real deeds — otherwise duplicate “just be nice.”

### Thread sight — all threads or strongest? (owner lean 2026-10-13)

**Not all at once** on crude inherit — avoids UI soup and keeps progression.

| Viewer tier | Crude inherit shows | Focused manual shows |
|-------------|---------------------|----------------------|
| **Lesser @3** | **Strongest 1** edge on subject + **count** of others (“+3 fainter threads”) |
| **Greater @4** | **Top 3** by strength; **kind** label (grudge/debt/favor); counterparty **silhouette** if not met |
| **Supreme crude** | Top 5 + **pair** edges between two subjects you select |
| **Art (manual)** | Full list above strength floor; **counterparty name**; org threads |

**Strength bands (UI colour / copy):**

| Band | Strength | Player reads |
|------|----------|--------------|
| **Faint** | 1 | “Something connects you” |
| **Clear** | 2 | Kind known (debt, favor, …) |
| **Strong** | 3–4 | Counterparty identifiable; feud tier visible |
| **Binding** | 5 | Clan/blood/oath — hard to sever |

Out of combat: sense on NPC in converse/explore. In combat: sense on foe shows **strongest hostile edge** only (who they’re fighting **for**, blood link hint).

### Verbs: reinforce · sever · (rare) faint-bind

| Verb | Crude inherit | Focused manual | Apex manual |
|------|---------------|----------------|-------------|
| **Reinforce** | **Greater @4** — wasteful | **Red Ledger**, **Grudge Needle** | **Blood Debt Orchestration** (multi-party) |
| **Sever** | — | **Thread Shears** (one edge, strength ≤3) | **Solitary Verdict** (network cut — isolation) |
| **Faint-bind** | — | optional — strength-1 only | almost never on inherit |

**Reinforce examples (your sketches):**

- **Ally:** talented elder passing through → reinforce **favor** edge → bias **linger** / offer teach beat (not slave — **appetite** check in thread engine).
- **Enemy indirect:** two rivals in same room → reinforce **mutual grudge** edge → **+heat** / **grudgeLevel** on existing thread — feud tier rises **without you entering combat**.

**Sever (late menace):** cut **protector / clan / ally** edges on target → retaliation **organizes slower** (assassination window). **Not** delete all consequences — **severed** sides may still act if incidents remain. Heavy **corruption** if severing merit-oath edges on innocents. Counter: peer Karma re-tie, temple rite.
