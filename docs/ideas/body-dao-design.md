## How Vessel Rules use Body Dao

```text
Walk body path (chamber, realms)     ← already under Body Dao purview
    → swear Vessel Rule                ← rule **adheres** to flesh (Body Dao witnesses)
    → flesh **settles** under law      ← adherence deepens (not intent meditation)
    → breach → sting / slip / regress  ← Body Dao punishes
    → **Crowned** adherence               ← rule **crowns** the vessel (Da Chi)
```

| Piece | Layer (taxonomy) |
|-------|------------------|
| **Body Dao** | Great Dao — **witnesses** oaths; rewards/punishes adherence |
| **Vessel Rule** | Personal oath **nested under** Body Dao |
| **Rule Adherence** | **Own progression system** — law settling into flesh ([`vessel-rules-design.md`](vessel-rules-design.md)); **not** Weapon Intent clone |
| **Saintly Flesh** | Body-path completion — *Crowned adherence at Saint plausible* |
| **Sword Dao + sword intent** | Different loop — qi resonance, imposed will on tool category |

**Crowned adherence:** Body Dao’s **final gift** — rule **crowns** the tempered vessel (冠律). Not manifested **Law**. See [`vessel-rules-design.md`](vessel-rules-design.md) § Crowned.

**Physical Dao (owner term):** same working lane as **Body Dao** in these docs until hanzi lock.

**Contrast Sword (loop, not tier count):** Sword Dao disciples **meditate intent** — impose will on qi through a weapon category. Body Dao disciples **swear** — rule **sticks**; power **unlocks from adherence**. Vajra Ridge inner court **settles** one law into the vessel for life.

**Player voice:** “Swore upon the flesh” / “body oath” — **not** UI popup “Body Dao layer 2.”

## The can of worms (parked — do not resolve in one pass)

**Owner note (2026-08-02):** the worm they care about later is **not** nested-oath mechanics — it is **why some daos feel cosmologically weighty** (alchemy, forging, body) while others are “just something to learn and wield.” **Not important right now.** Park in [`law-taxonomy.md`](law-taxonomy.md) § dao importance asymmetry.

### 1. Implicit vs explicit Body Dao

Does every tempering action **implicitly** invoke Body Dao, or only **oath + peak** rituals **name** it?

| Lean | Implication |
|------|-------------|
| **Implicit** | Outer court brawlers are **already** in purview; oath is **deepening** |
| **Explicit** | Only sworn inner court “truly” walks Body Dao — outer = technique only |

**Owner thread:** lean **implicit** — purview is **technical truth**, not sect marketing.

### 2. Dual-path cultivators

Qi-primary with body chamber, or body-primary with qi arts — **who witnesses** the oath?

- Park: oath binds **vessel track** only; Body Dao purview **does not** claim dantian
- Risk: qi-body saints break taxonomy if both paths peak

### 3. Saint vs Dao Manifestation

| Body | Qi (orthodox) |
|------|----------------|
| Saintly Flesh 圣体境 | Dao Manifestation idx 7 |
| Person: **Saint** 圣 | Person: **Immortal** 仙 |

Is Saint = **body-path embodied completion** (layer 3 for flesh lane only)? Or **different tier** entirely?

**Park:** Saint is **body Great Dao finisher** — parallel **completion**, not lesser Immortal.

### 4. Which axis?

Sword Dao compounds metal/wind metaphors. Body Dao — **yang vitality** lane? **Independent** “flesh axis”? **Sub-lane** under yang?

**Park:** do not bind to axis essay until owner cosmology pass — Body Dao is **path-first** (how mortals refine), axis is **infrastructure-first**.

### 5. Martial Intent relationship

Martial Intent = enlightenment **through impact** (fist). Is it **subset** of Body Dao purview or **sibling** skill gate?

**Lean:** **subset** — intent is how body **strikes**; Vessel Rule is how body **binds**; both under Body Dao.

### 6. Blood demon / forbidden physique

Forbidden practices **stain** or **bargain** — **not** Body Dao oath. Keep [`alignment-sacrilege-corruption.md`](alignment-sacrilege-corruption.md) separate.

### 7. NPC embodied body law

Can a dead Saint leave **fist-line stain** like Sword intent on a cliff?

**Park:** possible fiction; not required for player Vessel Rules.
